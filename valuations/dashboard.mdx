View a comprehensive overview of your organization's valuation data with visual charts and key metrics.

## Accessing the Dashboard

Navigate to **Valuations** → **Dashboard** from the sidebar to access your valuation overview.

<Note>
**Approved Valuations Only**: The dashboard only shows approved valuations. Draft valuations are not included in calculations or charts.
</Note>

## Empty State

If you don't have any approved valuations yet, you'll see a helpful prompt:

- **Icon**: Clipboard graphic
- **Message**: "No approved valuations"
- **Description**: "Create your first valuation to see insights here"
- **Action**: "Create Valuation" button linking to the valuation form

<Tip>
Create and approve your first valuation to unlock the dashboard visualizations!
</Tip>

## Metric Cards

Three key metric cards provide at-a-glance insights with trend indicators:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/valuations/dashboard/metric-cards.png" alt="Metric Cards" />
</Frame>

### Total Attention Valuation

Shows your organization's total combined valuation across all value types.

**Calculation**: Sales Value + Media Value + Brand Multiplier Impact

**Trend Indicator**: Compares to previous approved valuation
- **Green arrow ↑** - Increase from previous valuation
- **Red arrow ↓** - Decrease from previous valuation
- **Percentage** - Shows exact change percentage

<Info>
**Currency**: Displayed in your organization's base currency (set in Settings).
</Info>

### Total Reach

Aggregate of all media reach metrics across channels.

**Includes**:
- TV viewers
- Web TV views
- PR reach
- Digital advertising impressions
- Social media impressions
- Match attendance

**Trend Indicator**: Compares to previous approved valuation, showing percentage change

<Tip>
Total reach helps demonstrate sponsor visibility across all touchpoints.
</Tip>

### Brand Value Indicator

Average of your four brand indicators on a 1-10 scale.

**Calculation**: (Popularity + CSR + Culture + Loyalty) / 4

**Display**: Shows as `X.X/10` (e.g., `7.5/10`)

**Trend Indicator**: Compares to previous approved valuation, showing change in points

## Valuation Methods

## Notation Toggle

Button: "Show Full Notation" / "Show Abbreviated Notation" (top right)

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/valuations/dashboard/notation-toggle.png" alt="Notation Toggle" />
</Frame>

Switch between full numbers and abbreviated notation for better readability.

**Full Notation**:
- Shows complete numbers (e.g., "1,234,567")
- Best for precise values and accounting
- Default view

**Abbreviated Notation**:
- Shows shortened numbers with suffixes (e.g., "1.2M", "567K")
- Best for quick overviews and presentations
- Cleaner visual appearance

**How to Toggle**:
1. Look for the button in the top right corner of the dashboard
2. Click "Show Abbreviated Notation" to switch to shortened format
3. Click "Show Full Notation" to return to complete numbers

**Where It Applies**:
- Metric cards (Total Attention Valuation, Total Reach)
- All chart values and tooltips
- Breakdown chart labels
- Legend values

<Tip>
Use abbreviated notation when presenting to stakeholders for cleaner slides. Use full notation when preparing detailed financial reports.
</Tip>

**Title**: "Total Attention Valuation Breakdown"

A horizontal stacked bar chart showing the relative contribution of every value component.

### Value Components

The chart visualizes all sub-components in three color-coded groups:

**Sales (Blue Shades)**
- Merchandise revenue
- Matchday revenue
- Player sales value

**Media (Green Shades)**
- TV broadcasting
- Web TV
- PR & Press
- Digital advertising
- Social media
- Match & hospitality

**Brand Multiplier (Purple Shades)**
- Popularity impact
- CSR impact
- Culture impact
- Loyalty/Fans impact

<Tip>
**Interactive**: Hover over any segment to see the exact value, currency amount, and percentage of total.

### How to Read It

- **Width of each segment** = Relative contribution to total value
- **Color groups** = Easy identification of value type

  - Exact monetary value
  - Percentage of total valuation

<Info>

</Info>

## Brand Value Spider Chart

**Title**: "Brand Value Indicators"
**Subtitle**: Shows average brand value (e.g., "Average: 7.5/10")

A radar/spider chart showing your four brand indicators on a 1-10 scale.

### Indicators

All four indicators are plotted on a radial chart with a scale from 0 to 10:

- **Popularity** - Brand awareness and recognition (purple)
- **CSR** - Corporate Social Responsibility perception (dark purple)
- **Culture** - Brand culture and identity strength (light purple)
- **Fans/Loyalty** - Fan and stakeholder loyalty (very light purple)

### Features

- **Radial grid** - Shows scale from 0 to 10
- **Purple shading** - Filled area represents overall brand strength
- **Interactive tooltip** - Hover over corners to see exact values
- **Legend below** - Shows all four indicators with values (X/10)

### How to Read It

- **Larger shape** = Stronger overall brand value
- **Balanced shape** = Consistent brand strength across indicators
- **Uneven shape** = Identifies areas for brand development

<Tip>
A balanced spider chart (evenly shaped) indicates consistent brand strength. Use uneven shapes to identify areas for improvement.
</Tip>

## Valuation Over Time Chart

**Displays when**: You have 2 or more valuations

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/valuations/dashboard/valuation-over-time.png" alt="Valuation Over Time Chart" />
</Frame>

A stacked area chart showing valuation trends across time.

**Title**: "Valuation Over Time"

### What It Shows

Visualizes how your total valuation and its components have changed over time, with each valuation plotted chronologically.

**Chart Type**: Stacked area chart with smooth curves

**X-Axis**: Valuation dates (chronological order)
**Y-Axis**: Total valuation amount (in your base currency)

### Value Layers

The chart displays three stacked layers representing the main value categories:

**Sales Layer (Blue)**
- Bottom layer of the chart
- Includes merchandise, matchday, and player sales
- Foundation of your valuation

**Media Layer (Green)**
- Middle layer of the chart
- Includes TV, Web TV, PR, digital, social, and attendance
- Shows media value contribution over time

**Brand Multiplier Layer (Purple)**
- Top layer of the chart
- Includes popularity, CSR, culture, and loyalty impacts
- Represents brand value enhancement

### Features

- **Smooth curves** - Easy to follow trends and patterns
- **Interactive tooltips** - Hover over any point to see:
  - Exact date of valuation
  - Total valuation amount
  - Breakdown by value type (Sales, Media, Brand)
  - Individual component values
- **Legend below** - Shows all three value types with color indicators
- **Responsive design** - Adapts to mobile and desktop screens

### How to Read It

- **Upward slope** = Valuation growth over time
- **Downward slope** = Valuation decrease
- **Flat line** = Stable valuation
- **Layer thickness** = Contribution of each value type
- **Color changes** = Shifts in value mix (more media vs. sales, etc.)

### Visibility Requirements

<Note>
This chart **only appears** when you have **2 or more approved valuations**. Create regular valuations to unlock this trend visualization.
</Note>

<Tip>
Create valuations monthly or quarterly to build a meaningful trend history. The more data points, the more valuable the insights!
</Tip>

### Use Cases

**Sponsor Presentations**:
- Show growth trajectory to potential sponsors
- Demonstrate increasing media value over time
- Highlight consistent brand development

**Board Reporting**:
- Track progress against strategic goals
- Identify seasonal patterns or trends
- Report on valuation milestones

**Strategic Analysis**:
- Identify which value types are growing fastest
- Spot areas of decline requiring attention
- Correlate valuation changes with business decisions

<Info>
**Pro Tip**: If you see a sudden spike or drop, hover over that point to see the exact valuation details and identify what changed.
</Info>

## Cash Flow Visualization

**Plugin Required**: This chart requires the Cash Flow Visualization (Sankey) plugin (Professional/Enterprise plans). See [Platform → Plugins](/platform/plugins#cash-flow-visualization) for setup details.

**Displays when**: Cash Flow Visualization plugin is enabled in Platform → Plugins

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/valuations/dashboard/cash-flow-visualization.png" alt="Cash Flow Visualization" />
</Frame>

An interactive Sankey chart showing value flow from individual components to final total.

**Title**: "Cash Flow Visualization" (configurable in plugin settings)

### What It Shows

A Sankey (alluvial flow) diagram that visualizes how individual value components flow together to create your total valuation.

**Flow Direction**: Left to right
- **Left side**: Individual value components (merchandise, TV, social media, etc.)
- **Middle**: Category groupings (Sales, Media, Brand)
- **Right side**: Total Attention Valuation

### Visual Features

**Flow Bands**:
- Width represents the value amount
- Color matches the component category:
  - **Blue shades** - Sales components
  - **Green shades** - Media components
  - **Purple shades** - Brand components

**Interactive Elements**:
- **Hover over flows** - See exact value and percentage
- **Hover over nodes** - See total for that category
- **Smooth animations** - Flows animate on page load (configurable)

### Configuration Options

Customize the chart appearance in **Platform → Plugins → Cash Flow Visualization** settings:

**Chart Type**:
- Sankey (Alluvial Flow) - Recommended
- Waterfall
- Custom

**Appearance**:
- **Chart Height** - Pixels (default: 600px)
- **Color Scheme** - Default (multi-color), Blue, Green, Purple, Custom
- **Chart Position** - Before Sales, After Sales, Top of Page, Bottom of Page

**Display Options**:
- Show Legend (toggle)
- Show Tooltips (toggle)
- Show Labels (toggle)
- Enable Interactivity (toggle)
- Animation Duration (milliseconds)

<Tip>
Use the Sankey chart to show sponsors exactly where their sponsorship value comes from and how it flows into your total brand value.
</Tip>

### How to Read It

**Flow Width = Value Amount**:
- Wider flows = Higher value components
- Narrow flows = Lower value components

**Convergence Points**:
- Multiple flows merge into category totals (Sales, Media, Brand)
- All categories converge into final Total Attention Valuation

**Color Coding**:
- Quick visual identification of value types
- Easy to spot which categories dominate your valuation

### Enabling the Chart

<Note>
**Professional/Enterprise Required**: This chart requires the Cash Flow Visualization plugin, available on Professional and Enterprise plans.
</Note>

**Setup Steps**:
1. Navigate to **Platform → Plugins**
2. Find "Cash Flow Visualization (Sankey)" card
3. Click **"Enable"** button
4. (Optional) Click **gear icon** to configure appearance
5. Return to Dashboard to see the chart

<Info>
The chart appears on both the **Dashboard** and individual **Valuation Details** pages once enabled.
</Info>

## Common Questions

### Why Don't I See My Latest Valuation?

Make sure your valuation is **approved**. Draft valuations don't appear on the dashboard. Go to **Valuations** → **Valuations**, open your valuation, and click "Approve" to include it.

### Why Are Some Charts Empty?

Charts only show components with values greater than 0. If you didn't enter data for certain channels (e.g., no Web TV metrics), those segments won't appear.

### What Does "No Previous Data" Mean?

If you only have one approved valuation, there's no previous valuation to compare against. Trend indicators will show "No previous data" until you have at least two approved valuations.

### Can I Export Charts?

You can take screenshots of the dashboard or use your browser's print function. Future updates may include built-in export functionality.

<Tip>
Create valuations regularly (monthly or quarterly) to build trend history and unlock the full value of dashboard analytics.
</Tip>
