# Valuation Dashboard & Real-Time KPIs

  <img src="/images/valuations/dashboard/overview.png" alt="Valuation dashboard showing real-time analytics, KPI tracking, trend indicators, and visual charts for sponsorship value monitoring" />

**Access comprehensive valuation analytics through an intuitive dashboard** that transforms your sponsorship data into actionable insights. View real-time metrics, track performance trends, and analyze value distribution across sales, media, and brand channels with visual charts designed for data-driven decision making.

View a comprehensive overview of your organization's valuation data with visual charts and key metrics.

## Accessing the Dashboard

Navigate to **Valuations** → **Dashboard** from the sidebar to access your valuation overview.

<Note>
**Approved Valuations Only**: The dashboard only shows approved valuations. Draft valuations are not included in calculations or charts.
</Note>

## Empty State

If you don't have any approved valuations yet, you'll see a helpful prompt:

- **Icon**: Clipboard graphic
- **Message**: "No approved valuations"
- **Description**: "Create your first valuation to see insights here"
- **Action**: "Create Valuation" button linking to the valuation form

<Tip>
Create and approve your first valuation to unlock the dashboard visualizations!
</Tip>

## Metric Cards

Three key metric cards provide at-a-glance insights with trend indicators:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/valuations/dashboard/metric-cards.png" alt="Dashboard metric cards displaying total attention valuation, total reach analytics, and brand value KPI indicators with trend comparisons" />

### Total Attention Valuation

Shows your organization's total combined valuation across all value types.

**Calculation**: Sales Value + Media Value + Brand Multiplier Impact

**Trend Indicator**: Compares to previous approved valuation
- **Green arrow ↑** - Increase from previous valuation
- **Red arrow ↓** - Decrease from previous valuation
- **Percentage** - Shows exact change percentage

<Info>
**Currency**: Displayed in your organization's base currency (set in Settings).
</Info>

### Total Reach

Aggregate of all media reach metrics across channels.

**Includes**:
- TV viewers
- Web TV views
- PR reach
- Digital advertising impressions
- Social media impressions
- Match attendance

**Trend Indicator**: Compares to previous approved valuation, showing percentage change

<Tip>
Total reach helps demonstrate sponsor visibility across all touchpoints.
</Tip>

### Brand Value Indicator

Average of your four brand indicators on a 1-10 scale.

**Calculation**: (Popularity + CSR + Culture + Loyalty) / 4

**Display**: Shows as `X.X/10` (e.g., `7.5/10`)

**Trend Indicator**: Compares to previous approved valuation, showing change in points

## Valuation Methods

## Notation Toggle

Button: "Show Full Notation" / "Show Abbreviated Notation" (top right)

Switch between full numbers and abbreviated notation for better readability.

**Full Notation**:
- Shows complete numbers (e.g., "1,234,567")
- Best for precise values and accounting
- Default view

**Abbreviated Notation**:
- Shows shortened numbers with suffixes (e.g., "1.2M", "567K")
- Best for quick overviews and presentations
- Cleaner visual appearance

**How to Toggle**:
1. Look for the button in the top right corner of the dashboard
2. Click "Show Abbreviated Notation" to switch to shortened format
3. Click "Show Full Notation" to return to complete numbers

**Where It Applies**:
- Metric cards (Total Attention Valuation, Total Reach)
- All chart values and tooltips
- Breakdown chart labels
- Legend values

<Tip>
Use abbreviated notation when presenting to stakeholders for cleaner slides. Use full notation when preparing detailed financial reports.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I access the valuation dashboard?

Navigate to **Valuations** → **Dashboard** from the sidebar. You need at least one approved valuation to see dashboard analytics and KPI tracking visualizations.

### Why don't I see my latest valuation on the dashboard?

Make sure your valuation is **approved**. Draft valuations don't appear on the dashboard. Go to **Valuations** → **Valuations**, open your valuation, and click "Approve" to include it in the analytics overview.

### Why are some dashboard charts empty?

Charts only show components with values greater than 0. If you didn't enter data for certain channels (e.g., no Web TV metrics), those segments won't appear in the data visualization.

### What does "No Previous Data" mean on trend indicators?

If you only have one approved valuation, there's no previous valuation to compare against for trend analysis. Trend indicators will show "No previous data" until you have at least two approved valuations.

### Can I export dashboard charts for presentations?

You can take screenshots of the dashboard or use your browser's print function for stakeholder presentations. Future updates may include built-in export functionality for analytics reports.

### How do I switch between full and abbreviated notation?

Click the "Show Abbreviated Notation" or "Show Full Notation" button in the top right corner. Abbreviated notation displays values like "1.2M" while full notation shows complete numbers like "1,234,567".

### What metrics are included in Total Reach calculations?

Total Reach aggregates TV viewers, Web TV views, PR reach, digital advertising impressions, social media impressions, and match attendance. Social followers and engagements are excluded to avoid double-counting.

### How often should I create valuations for trend analysis?

Create valuations regularly (monthly or quarterly) to build trend history and unlock the full value of dashboard analytics and KPI tracking over time.

### Can I customize the dashboard currency display?

Yes, the dashboard displays values in your organization's base currency, which you can set in **Settings** → **Organisation** → **General Settings**.

### How does the brand value indicator affect my total valuation?

The Brand Value Indicator (calculated from Popularity, CSR, Culture, and Loyalty) acts as a multiplier on your combined sales and media value. Higher brand scores amplify your total attention valuation, while lower scores reduce it. The indicator helps quantify the impact of brand strength on overall sponsorship value.
