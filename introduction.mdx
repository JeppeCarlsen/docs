## Welcome to Valiyou™ Knowledge Center

Valiyou is the **all-in-one platform** for sports marketing and sponsorship management. We combine valuation science with sponsor relationship management to help sports organizations and brands maximize the value of their partnerships.

### Key Features

- **Sponsorship Valuation**: Data-driven valuations based on reach, engagement, and brand visibility
- **Sponsor Management**: CRM for managing sponsor relationships and contracts
- **Package Builder**: Create professional sponsorship packages and proposals
- **Inventory Management**: Track and allocate sponsorship assets
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time insights into sponsorship performance
- **Team Collaboration**: Invite team members with role-based permissions
- **API Access**: Integrate Valiyou with your existing tools
