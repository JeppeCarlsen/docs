## Platform Overview

Valiyou helps sports clubs and commercial teams:

- 📊 **Measure** - Calculate accurate sponsorship valuations based on sales, media, and brand metrics
- 🤝 **Manage** - Track sponsors, build packages, and send proposals with e-signatures
- 📈 **Grow** - Use AI-powered insights and analytics to optimize sponsorship revenue
