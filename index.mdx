## The Problem

### A $148B market without a pricing system

Before Google and Facebook, digital advertising was sold like billboards – based on placement and negotiation. Then CPM was introduced, and digital advertising became finance.

**Sponsorship never had its CPM moment.**

Today, sponsorship is a **$148 billion global market** where assets are still valued through subjective estimates, outdated benchmarks and opaque reporting models. The result is a massive inefficiency:

- **Rights holders cannot prove what they sell**
- **Brands cannot clearly understand what they buy**
- **The mid-market (99% of rights holders) operates without infrastructure**

They are **asset-rich, but data-poor**.

---

## The Solution

### The pricing layer sponsorship never had

**Valiyou is not another analytics tool. Valiyou is market infrastructure.**

We provide the standardized pricing layer that allows sponsorship assets to be valued, packaged and traded with financial logic.

Our platform **translates attention into equity**. Likes, views, attendance and engagement are converted into financial value (EUR / USD / DKK), creating a common commercial language between rights holders and brands.

**This transforms sponsorship from negotiated art into structured finance.**

## Product

### From valuation to deal signed

Valiyou is built as a **commercial operating system for sponsorship**. The platform connects three integrated layers:

### 1. The Valuation Engine — The Glass Box

The foundation of the platform.

Valiyou ingests verified data from social platform reports and physical attendance to calculate real commercial value.

**Every calculation is transparent.** Users can see exactly why an asset is priced the way it is.

Values update dynamically as attention changes, and users can benchmark themselves against anonymized market standards.

**This is not a score. It is a pricing engine.**

### 2. The Package & Proposal Builder — The Deal Layer

This is where data becomes products.

Users build sponsorship packages by combining assets into structured commercial offerings. As components are added, prices update in real time based on the valuation engine.

Packages instantly generate professional, white-label proposals and ROI projections.

**This moves sponsorship sales from Excel and PDFs into live commercial systems.**

### 3. The Reporting Layer — Proof of performance

After deals are signed, Valiyou tracks delivery.

Campaigns are measured automatically against promised KPIs, and sponsor-ready reports are generated with one click.

**This closes the commercial loop** and feeds new performance data back into the pricing engine.

## Why We Win

### Pre-deal pricing, not post-deal reporting

Most sponsorship platforms measure what happened yesterday.

**Valiyou prices what can be sold tomorrow.**

We are the only platform built around **pre-deal commercial valuation** rather than post-deal exposure reporting.

This creates three structural advantages:

### 1. Category ownership

Valiyou defines the pricing layer. Not CRM. Not reporting. Not monitoring.

**Pricing is where markets form.**

### 2. The currency flywheel

Every valuation strengthens our benchmarks. Better benchmarks strengthen the currency. A stronger currency drives contractual usage. Contractual usage drives standardization.

**More valuations → better benchmarks → stronger currency → standardization.**

This data network effect is our long-term defensibility.

### 3. A commercial operating system

Valiyou is not a single-purpose tool.

From valuation to package creation, from proposal to performance reporting, the platform covers the full commercial lifecycle.

**This creates high switching costs and positions Valiyou as infrastructure rather than software.**

## Business Model

### Pricing for adoption, not extraction

Currencies only become standards when they are accessible.

Valiyou operates a scalable **SaaS model** designed for high-volume adoption:

- **Starter tier** for influencers and athletes
- **Professional tier** for clubs and agencies
- **Enterprise tier** for brands, leagues and federations

Pricing is intentionally positioned below legacy enterprise systems to remove friction, drive volume and accelerate currency formation.

Long-term upside includes **data licensing, transaction layers and financial services** built on top of the valuation infrastructure.
