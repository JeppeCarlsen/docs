## Why Valiyou?

### Sponsorship pricing has been broken

Sponsorship deals are often based on gut feeling rather than data. Rights holders struggle to justify their pricing, and brands can't compare offers objectively.

Valiyou solves this by providing:

- **Data-driven valuations** based on verified reach, engagement, and brand visibility
- **Transparent pricing** that both parties can understand and trust
- **Professional proposals** that close deals faster

---

## How It Works

### Three simple steps

1. **Connect your data** - Link your social media accounts and import audience metrics
2. **Get your valuation** - Our engine calculates the commercial value of your sponsorship assets
3. **Create proposals** - Build professional packages and send branded proposals to sponsors

## Key Features

### Everything you need to manage sponsorships

**Valuation Engine**
Calculate the commercial value of your sponsorship assets based on reach, engagement, and brand visibility. All calculations are transparent and benchmarked against market standards.

**Package Builder**
Combine multiple assets into sponsorship packages with real-time pricing. Create professional, branded proposals that you can send directly to sponsors.

**Performance Reporting**
Track campaign delivery and generate sponsor-ready reports with one click. Measure actual performance against promised KPIs.

## Who Uses Valiyou?

Valiyou is trusted by sports organizations, creators, and brands across Europe.

**Sports Organizations**
Professional and semi-professional clubs use Valiyou to value their sponsorship assets and create data-backed proposals.

**Creators & Influencers**
Content creators use Valiyou to price brand partnerships based on their actual reach and engagement.

**Brands & Agencies**
Marketing teams use Valiyou to evaluate sponsorship opportunities and compare offers objectively.
