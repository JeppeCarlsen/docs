## Frequently Asked Questions

### What analytics metrics are available?

The analytics dashboard will track sponsorship revenue, package performance, sponsor engagement, proposal acceptance rates, email open rates, response times, and custom KPIs. You can view metrics by time period, sponsor tier, package type, and custom segments.

### How do I track sponsorship ROI?

ROI tracking compares actual revenue against projected targets, measures package profitability by analyzing cost-plus margins, tracks proposal conversion rates, and monitors sponsor lifetime value. Export reports to analyze return on investment across different sponsor tiers and package types.

### Can I export analytics data?

Yes, you can export analytics data as PDF reports for presentations, CSV files for custom analysis in Excel, scheduled email reports sent automatically, and custom report formats with selected metrics. All export options preserve data visualization and formatting.

### What permissions are needed for analytics?

You need either Analytics role permission or Admin access to view the analytics dashboard. Analytics role users can view all metrics, export reports, and schedule automated reports. Other roles cannot access the analytics module.

### How often is analytics data updated?

Analytics data updates in real-time when sponsors approve or reject proposals, when new packages are created or sent, and when sponsor engagement events occur (email opens, link clicks). Revenue metrics refresh hourly, and historical trend data updates daily.

### Can I compare performance across time periods?

Yes, the analytics dashboard supports month-over-month comparisons, quarter-over-quarter analysis, year-over-year trends, and custom date range comparisons. Visualizations show growth percentages and variance indicators for easy trend identification.

### What visualizations are available?

The analytics dashboard includes revenue trend line charts, sponsor tier pie charts, proposal funnel visualizations, monthly revenue bar charts, engagement heatmaps, coverage percentage histograms, and custom KPI gauges. All charts are interactive and exportable.

### How do I track proposal acceptance rates?

Proposal acceptance rates are calculated by dividing approved proposals by total sent proposals, segmented by sponsor tier, package type, time period, and sales representative. The proposal funnel visualization shows conversion at each stage from draft to approval.

### Can I schedule automated reports?

Yes, you can schedule automated analytics reports to be emailed daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly. Choose from executive summary, revenue report, engagement report, or custom report formats. Recipients can be team members, stakeholders, or board members.

### Is analytics available on all plans?

No, analytics features require Professional or Enterprise plans. Starter plan users cannot access the analytics module. Upgrade to Professional or Enterprise to unlock revenue tracking, performance metrics, engagement insights, and custom reporting capabilities.
