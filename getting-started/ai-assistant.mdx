## Your AI Assistant

Every Valiyou user has an AI assistant built into the platform. Click the sparkle icon in the bottom-right corner of any page to open the chat.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/getting-started/ai-chat-open.png"
    alt="Valiyou AI chat panel open on the dashboard, showing the sparkle icon and chat interface"
  />
</Frame>

The assistant knows your organization's data, industry, custom labels and current page. It uses your organization's own terminology — if you renamed "Sponsor" to "Brand", the assistant uses "Brand" too.

## What the assistant can do

<CardGroup cols="2">
  <Card title="Answer questions" icon="message-question">
    Ask about your valuations, sponsors, packages or inventory — the assistant looks up live data and answers in context.
  </Card>

<Card title="Fill forms" icon="pen-to-square">
  Say "create a sponsor called Heineken" and the assistant opens the form and fills the fields. You
  always press Save.
</Card>

<Card title="Navigate" icon="compass">
  Say "go to Reports" or "show me the latest valuation" and the assistant navigates for you.
</Card>

  <Card title="Generate insights" icon="sparkles">
    On the Reports page, click "Get AI Key Insights" for automatic analysis of your selected valuations.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Example conversations

Here are things you can ask the assistant:

### Valuations

- "What is our total valuation?"
- "Compare the last two valuations"
- "How has our media value changed?"
- "What are our TV viewers for the latest valuation?"

### Sponsors & Packages

- "Create a new sponsor called Nike"
- "How many sponsors do we have?"
- "What packages are in draft?"
- "Show me the Heineken sponsor page"

### Reports & Insights

- "Go to Reports"
- "What does the brand multiplier mean?"
- "Explain the valuation methodology"

### Navigation

- "Go to Settings"
- "Open the inventory"
- "Show me my profile"

<Tip>
  The assistant understands both English and Danish. It responds in the same language you write in.
</Tip>

## How it works

1. **Click the sparkle icon** in the bottom-right corner (or the chat panel header if already open)
2. **Type your question** or instruction
3. **The assistant responds** — it may look up data, navigate to a page, or open a form
4. **You stay in control** — the assistant never saves, approves or sends anything without you pressing the button

## AI Key Insights on Reports

On the **Reports** page, select two or more valuations and click **"Get AI Key Insights"**. The assistant analyzes your data and generates 3–5 actionable insights about trends, patterns and recommendations.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/getting-started/ai-insights-reports.png"
    alt="AI Key Insights section on the Reports page showing three generated insights with positive, neutral and negative indicators"
  />
</Frame>

No setup required — insights use Valiyou's built-in AI. Organizations on Professional or Enterprise can connect their own AI provider via [Private AI Connection](/platform/plugins/ai-insights) for data sovereignty.

## Privacy

- Messages and on-screen data are processed by Anthropic (USA)
- Anthropic does not use API data for model training
- Full details: [Trust Center → Sub-Processors](/trust-center/sub-processors)
- Organizations that need data to stay under their own data processing agreement can use [Private AI Connection](/platform/plugins/ai-insights)

## Limits

- **100 AI operations per organization per day** (shared between chat messages and Report insights)
- Organizations with [Private AI Connection](/platform/plugins/ai-insights) have no limit
- The assistant can read your data but cannot save, approve, send or delete anything — you always confirm

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the AI assistant available on all plans?">
    Yes. Every plan includes the AI assistant and AI Key Insights on Reports. The daily limit of 100 operations applies to all plans on Valiyou's built-in AI.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can the assistant change my data?">
  No. The assistant can open forms and fill fields, but you always press Save, Approve or Send. It
  never makes changes without your confirmation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the assistant know my organization's custom labels?">
  Yes. If you renamed fields in Settings (e.g. "Sponsor" → "Brand"), the assistant uses your custom
  terms automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use the assistant on mobile?">
  Yes. The chat panel works on mobile as a bottom sheet. Drag down to dismiss, tap the sparkle icon
  to reopen. Your conversation is preserved.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when we reach the daily limit?">
  You see a message asking to try again tomorrow. The limit resets 24 hours after the first
  operation. Connect your own AI provider via Private AI Connection to remove the limit entirely.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is my data processed?">
    Messages are processed by Anthropic in the USA. Anthropic does not use API data for model training. Organizations needing EU data residency can use Private AI Connection with a European AI provider (Azure OpenAI EU, Google Vertex EU, Mistral).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
