## Step 1: Create Your Account

### Sign Up

1. Visit [valiyou.com/auth/signup](https://valiyou.com/auth/signup)
2. Choose your preferred sign-up method:
   - **Email and password** (recommended)
   - Google Sign-In
   - Microsoft Account
   - LinkedIn
   - Salesforce

<Tip>
**Recommended**: Use email and password for the most flexible authentication. You can link additional accounts later.
</Tip>

### Verify Your Email

1. Check your inbox for verification email
2. Click the verification link
3. You'll be redirected to complete setup

<Warning>
**Check Spam Folder**: Verification emails sometimes land in spam. If you don't receive it within 5 minutes, check there first.
</Warning>

## Step 2: Set Up Your Organization

After logging in, you'll go through a 5-step onboarding wizard to set up your organization.

### Organization Details

Enter your company or club information:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="CVR/VAT Number" icon="building">
    Your company registration number. This is used for invoicing and legal compliance.

    **Format**: DK12345678 (Denmark) or equivalent for your country
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Organization Name" icon="signature">
    Your official company or club name as it appears on legal documents.

    **Example**: "FC Copenhagen" or "Brøndby IF Commercial"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Address" icon="location-dot">
    Complete address including:
    - Street name and number
    - Postal code
    - City
    - Country

    **Used for**: Invoices, legal documents, and correspondence
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Upload Your Logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Avatar Circle">
    Click the large circular placeholder in the organization setup
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Image">
    Choose a file from your computer (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP under 5MB)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crop & Adjust">
    Use the crop tool to frame your logo properly
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Your logo will appear on reports, proposals, and throughout the platform
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set Base Currency

Choose your preferred currency for all financial calculations:

- **EUR** (Euro) — Most European clubs
- **DKK** (Danish Krone) — Danish organizations
- **USD** (US Dollar) — American organizations
- **GBP** (British Pound) — UK organizations
- And 20+ more currencies

<Info>
**Currency Impact**: This affects all valuations, sponsorship packages, and financial reports. Choose the currency your sponsors typically use.
</Info>

## Step 5: Invite Your Team

Get your team onboard to start collaborating.

### Send Invitations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Users">
    Go to **Platform** → **Users**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    Click **"Invite User"** button (top right)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Email">
    Type your team member's email address
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Roles">
    Choose appropriate permissions from the tree
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    Click **"Send Invitation"** — they'll receive an email
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Recommended Roles

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Admin" icon="user-shield">
    **Grant to**: Executive leadership, team leads

    **Access**: Full access to everything including billing, settings, and user management

    **Auto-grants**: All sub-permissions automatically
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reports + Valuations" icon="chart-line">
    **Grant to**: Analysts, commercial staff

    **Access**: Create/edit valuations, view analytics, generate reports

    **Permissions**: Edit Sales, Edit Marketing, Edit Brand, Reports
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sponsorships" icon="handshake">
    **Grant to**: Sales team, partnership managers

    **Access**: Manage sponsors, packages, proposals

    **Permissions**: Sponsors (if available on plan)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="View Only" icon="eye">
    **Grant to**: Board members, stakeholders

    **Access**: View reports and dashboards but cannot edit

    **Permissions**: View Sales, View Marketing, Reports
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
**Principle of Least Privilege**: Start with minimal permissions and add more as needed. It's easier to grant access later than to revoke it.
</Tip>

<Note>
**For Detailed Permissions Guide**: See [Getting Started → Invite Team Members](/getting-started/invite-team-members) or [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management).
</Note>

## Common Questions

### How long does onboarding take?

**Minimum**: 5 minutes (account, organization, basic profile)
**Recommended**: 15-20 minutes (includes team invitations and first valuation or sponsor setup)
**Full setup**: 30-45 minutes (all settings configured, team onboarded, first data entered)

### Do I need to complete everything in one session?

No! Your progress is automatically saved. You can:
- Log out and continue later
- Invite team members to help complete setup
- Start using Valiyou with basic setup and refine later

### What if I make a mistake during setup?

Everything can be changed later:
- Organization details → [Platform → Organization Settings](/platform/organization-settings)
- Profile settings → [Platform → User Profile](/platform/user-profile)
- Plan & billing → [Platform → Billing](/platform/billing)
- Team members → [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management)

### Can I skip the payment step?

No, a valid payment method is required to use Valiyou. However:
- You can start with the Starter plan and upgrade later
- You can cancel anytime (no long-term commitment on monthly plans)
- Annual plans offer 16.67% savings

### What happens after my team accepts invitations?

Team members will:
1. Receive invitation email
2. Click link to accept
3. Log in (or create account if new)
4. Automatically join your organization
5. See content based on their permissions

You'll see them move from "Pending" to "Active" status in [Platform → Users](/platform/team-management).
