Welcome to Valiyou! This guide will help you get up and running in just 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Visit [valiyou.com/auth/signup](https://valiyou.com/auth/signup) and create your account using:
    - Email and password
    - Google Sign-In
    - Microsoft Account
    - LinkedIn
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to verify your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Organization">
    Enter your organization details:
    - Organization name
    - Industry
    - Country
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Create Your First Valuation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Valuations">
    Click **Valuations** in the sidebar, then click **New Valuation**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Basic Info">
    - Name: e.g., "Q4 2025 Valuation"
    - Date: Select valuation date
    - Status: Choose "Draft" for now
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Metrics">
    Input your data for:
    - **Sales**: Revenue from sponsorships
    - **Media**: Impressions and reach
    - **Brand**: Brand exposure value
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate & Save">
    Click **Calculate Value** to see your total attention value, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **Congratulations!** You've created your first valuation.
</Check>
