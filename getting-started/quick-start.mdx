Welcome to Valiyou! This guide will help you get up and running in just 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Visit [valiyou.com/auth/signup](https://valiyou.com/auth/signup) and create your account.

### Signup Methods

We recommend signing up with **email and password** for the best experience. Alternative options include:

- **Email and password** (recommended)
- Google Sign-In
- Microsoft Account
- LinkedIn
- Salesforce

### Email Verification

After signing up with email, you'll receive a verification email. Click the link in the email to verify your account before proceeding.

## Step 2: Set Up Your Organization

Once logged in, you'll be guided through a 5-step onboarding process to set up your organization.

### Organization Details

Enter your organization information:

- **CVR/VAT Number** - Your company registration number
- **Organization Name** - Your company or club name
- **Address** - Street name, number, postal code, city, and country

### Choose Your Plan

Select the plan that best fits your needs:

- **Starter** - Perfect for small clubs getting started with valuations
- **Professional** - For growing organizations with advanced needs (most popular)
- **Enterprise** - Unlimited power for large organizations

Choose between **monthly** or **annual** billing (save 16.67% with annual).

### Select Add-ons

Enhance your experience with optional add-ons:

- **AI Insights** - Automatically finds patterns, deviations, and trends in your reports
- **Sankey Chart** - Visualizes value flows from source to destination

### Review & Payment

Review your selections, apply any discount codes, and complete payment through Stripe's secure checkout.

## What's Next?

Once your organization is set up, you'll be redirected to your dashboard where you can:

- Create your first valuation
- Invite team members
- Explore sponsorship management tools
- Access platform settings
