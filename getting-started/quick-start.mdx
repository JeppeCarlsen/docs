# Quick Start Guide

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/quick-start.png" alt="Valiyou quick start onboarding dashboard showing step-by-step setup guide for new sports organizations" />
</Frame>

**Get your sports organization up and running on Valiyou in just 5 minutes with our streamlined onboarding process.** This comprehensive getting started guide walks you through account creation, organization setup, subscription plan selection, and launching your first sponsorship valuation. Follow our platform tour to unlock the full potential of data-driven sponsorship management and valuation analytics.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Visit [valiyou.io/auth/signup](https://valiyou.io/auth/signup) and create your account.

### Signup Methods

We recommend signing up with **email and password** for the best experience. Alternative options include:

- **Email and password** (recommended)
- Google Sign-In
- Microsoft Account
- LinkedIn
- Salesforce

### Email Verification

After signing up with email, you'll receive a verification email. Click the link in the email to verify your account before proceeding.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/verify-email.png" alt="Email verification page for Valiyou account activation showing confirmation message and security verification process" />
</Frame>

## Step 2: Set Up Your Organization

Once logged in, you'll be guided through a 5-step onboarding process to set up your organization.

### Organization Details

Enter your organization information:

- **CVR/VAT Number** - Your company registration number
- **Organization Name** - Your company or club name
- **Address** - Street name, number, postal code, city, and country

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/step-organization.png" alt="Organization details form in Valiyou onboarding showing fields for company registration, name, and address information" />
</Frame>

### Choose Your Plan

Select the plan that best fits your needs:

- **Starter** - Perfect for small clubs getting started with valuations
- **Professional** - For growing organizations with advanced needs (most popular)
- **Enterprise** - Unlimited power for large organizations

Choose between **monthly** or **annual** billing (save 16.67% with annual).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/step-plans.png" alt="Valiyou pricing plans comparison showing Starter, Professional, and Enterprise subscription options with features and monthly or annual billing" />
</Frame>

### Select Add-ons

Enhance your experience with optional add-ons:

- **AI Insights** - Automatically finds patterns, deviations, and trends in your reports
- **Sankey Chart** - Visualizes value flows from source to destination

  <img src="/images/getting-started/step-addons.png" alt="Valiyou add-ons selection page displaying AI Insights and Sankey Chart premium features for enhanced analytics" />

Review your selections and apply any discount codes if you have them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does the onboarding process take?">
    The entire Valiyou onboarding process takes approximately 5 minutes to complete. This includes creating your account, verifying your email, entering organization details, selecting a subscription plan, choosing add-ons, and completing payment. Once finished, you'll have immediate access to your dashboard and can start creating valuations right away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my subscription plan after setup?">
    Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your subscription plan at any time from **Platform → Billing → Plans**. When upgrading, changes take effect immediately and you'll be prorated for the remaining billing period. When downgrading, changes apply at the end of your current billing cycle to ensure you get full value from your current plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods does Valiyou accept?">
    Valiyou uses Stripe for secure payment processing and accepts all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, and various local payment methods depending on your region. All payments are PCI-compliant and encrypted. You can manage payment methods in **Platform → Billing → Payment Methods**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial available?">
    Valiyou offers a trial period for new users to explore the platform features. Check our pricing page or contact our sales team for current trial offers. During the trial, you'll have access to core features including valuations, sponsorship management, and basic reporting to evaluate if Valiyou fits your organization's needs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I skip email verification?">
    Email verification is required to access your Valiyou account fully. Without verification, you may have limited access to features and won't receive important notifications about valuations, team collaboration, or billing updates. If you didn't receive the verification email, check your spam folder or request a new verification email from the login page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Valiyou without entering a VAT/CVR number?">
    The VAT/CVR number is required for organizations subject to VAT regulations and helps ensure proper invoicing. If your organization is not VAT-registered (such as some non-profits or organizations in certain jurisdictions), you can enter "N/A" or contact support for assistance with account setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's included in each subscription plan?">
    The Starter plan includes essential valuation tools and basic reporting for small teams. Professional adds advanced analytics, unlimited valuations, team collaboration features, and priority support. Enterprise provides everything in Professional plus custom integrations, dedicated support, unlimited users, and advanced security features. View detailed feature comparisons on our pricing page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are the AI Insights and Sankey Chart add-ons worth it?">
    AI Insights automatically analyzes your valuation data to identify trends, outliers, and opportunities that might otherwise be missed, saving hours of manual analysis. The Sankey Chart provides visual flow diagrams showing how sponsorship value moves through your organization, making presentations to stakeholders more impactful. Both add-ons are particularly valuable for Professional and Enterprise users managing complex sponsorship portfolios.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription at any time?">
    Yes, you can cancel your Valiyou subscription at any time from **Platform → Billing → Cancel Subscription**. For monthly plans, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. For annual plans, you'll retain access until the end of your paid year. You can export all your data before cancellation to ensure you don't lose any valuations or reports.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get help if I get stuck during setup?">
    Valiyou offers multiple support channels during onboarding. Access our comprehensive knowledge base, watch video tutorials, use the in-app chat support for instant help, or email support@valiyou.com. Professional and Enterprise customers also have access to dedicated onboarding specialists who can provide personalized setup assistance and training sessions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
