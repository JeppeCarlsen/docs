Welcome to Valiyou! This guide will help you get up and running in just 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Visit [valiyou.com/auth/signup](https://valiyou.com/auth/signup) and create your account using:
    - Email and password
    - Google Sign-In
    - Microsoft Account
    - LinkedIn
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to verify your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Organization">
    Enter your organization details:
    - Organization name
    - Industry
    - Country
  </Step>
</Steps>
