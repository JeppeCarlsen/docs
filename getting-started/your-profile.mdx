Access your profile settings by clicking your avatar in the top right corner and selecting "Profile".

## Profile Avatar

Your avatar appears in the top bar, comments, and team member lists.

### Upload Avatar

1. Click the **avatar circle** at the top of your Profile page
2. Select an image file (max 2MB)
3. **Crop and adjust** the avatar using the crop tool
4. Click "Save" to apply

<Tip>
**Drag & Drop**: You can also drag an image file directly onto the avatar circle to upload.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG. Images are automatically cropped to a circle format.
</Info>

### Edit Existing Avatar

To edit your current avatar:
1. Click your avatar circle
2. The crop modal opens with your current image
3. Adjust the crop area
4. Click "Save" to update

### Remove Avatar

To remove your avatar:
1. Click your avatar circle
2. Click "Delete" in the crop modal
3. Confirm removal

If you don't have an avatar, your initials will be displayed instead.

## Personal Information

Update your personal details and language preferences.

### Name

Your full name appears in:
- Team member lists
- Comments and activity logs
- Email notifications
- Invitation emails

### Email Address

Your email is used for:
- Account login
- Important notifications
- Password reset
- Team invitations

<Warning>
**Email Change Verification**: When you change your email address, you must verify the new email before it becomes active. Check your inbox for a verification email.
</Warning>

### Language

Choose your preferred language for the platform interface:
- **English** - Full platform in English
- **Dansk** - Full platform in Danish

The language setting applies to:
- All menus and navigation
- Form labels and buttons
- System messages
- Report labels

<Note>
Language changes apply immediately and affect only your account, not your organization.
</Note>

Click **"Save Personal Info"** to apply changes.

## Linked Accounts

View all social accounts connected to your Valiyou profile.

### Supported Providers

- **Google** - Sign in with Google account
- **Apple** - Sign in with Apple ID
- **Microsoft** - Sign in with Microsoft account
- **Facebook** - Sign in with Facebook account
- **LinkedIn** - Sign in with LinkedIn account
- **Email/Password** - Traditional credential login

### How Linked Accounts Work

- You can link multiple sign-in methods to the same account
- All linked accounts give access to the same profile and organizations
- "Connected" badge indicates active links
- Each provider is shown with its official logo and branding

<Info>
**Primary Email**: Your primary email address is determined by the first account you used to sign up, but you can change it in Personal Information.
</Info>

## Security

<Note>
**Password Section**: Only visible if you have email/password (credential) login enabled.
</Note>

### Change Password

1. Enter your **Current Password**
2. Enter your **New Password** (minimum 6 characters)
3. **Confirm New Password** (must match)
4. Click **"Save Password"**

<Warning>
**Password Requirements**: Passwords must be at least 6 characters long. Use a strong, unique password to protect your account.
</Warning>

### Password Change Process

- Your current password is verified before allowing changes
- All validation is performed before saving
- You remain logged in after password change
- Other active sessions are NOT revoked (you stay logged in on other devices)

**Common Errors:**
- "Current password is incorrect" - Double-check your existing password
- "Passwords do not match" - Confirm password must exactly match new password
- "Password must be at least 6 characters" - Use a longer password

## Danger Zone: Delete Account

<Warning>
**Permanent Action**: Account deletion cannot be undone and will permanently remove all your data after 30 days.
</Warning>

### What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following are permanently removed:
- Your user profile and avatar
- All team memberships
- Access to all organizations and data

### Deletion Requirements

You **cannot** delete your account if:
- You are the only admin of one or more organizations
- You need to transfer admin rights or delete organizations first

### Deletion Process

1. Click **"Delete Account"** in the Danger Zone
2. System checks if account can be deleted
3. If you're the only admin of organizations, you'll see an error listing which ones
4. Type **DELETE** (in capital letters) to confirm
5. Your account is marked for deletion
6. **30-day grace period** begins
7. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted

<Info>
**30-Day Grace Period**: You have 30 days to cancel the deletion by contacting support. After 30 days, deletion is permanent.
</Info>

### Before Deleting Your Account

Make sure you:
- Transfer admin rights to another team member for organizations you manage
- Or delete organizations you no longer need
- Save any important data or reports you want to keep
- Inform your team members of the change

**Alternatives to Deletion:**
- If you just want to leave an organization, ask an admin to remove you instead
- If you want to take a break, you can simply log out (no deletion needed)
- If you're unhappy with the service, contact support@valiyou.com for help

## Troubleshooting

### Avatar Not Updating

If your avatar doesn't update after upload:
1. **Check file size** - Must be under 2MB
2. **Check file format** - Use JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, or SVG
3. **Refresh the page** - Click your avatar in the top bar to see the update
4. **Check browser cache** - Clear cache if you see the old avatar

### Email Verification Not Received

If you don't receive the email verification:
1. **Check spam folder** - Verification emails may be filtered
2. **Wait a few minutes** - Email delivery can be delayed
3. **Check email address** - Make sure it's typed correctly
4. **Request new verification** - Change email again to trigger a new email

### Password Change Failed

If password change fails:
1. **Verify current password** - Make sure you're entering the right password
2. **Check requirements** - New password must be 6+ characters
3. **Match confirmation** - Confirm password must exactly match new password
4. **Try again** - Close any modals and try the process again

### Cannot Delete Account

If you cannot delete your account:
1. **Check admin status** - You may be the only admin of organizations
2. **Transfer admin rights** - Go to Settings → Users and promote another member
3. **Or delete organizations** - Go to Settings → Advanced → Delete Organisation
4. **Try again** - Once organizations are handled, deletion will work
