# Your Profile & Account Settings

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/your-profile.png" alt="Valiyou user profile settings page showing personal information, avatar customization, and account preferences dashboard" />
</Frame>

**Your Valiyou user profile is the central hub for managing personal preferences, account settings, and profile customization across your organization.** This comprehensive guide covers everything from uploading a professional avatar and updating contact information to changing language preferences and managing account security. Learn how to personalize your profile to enhance collaboration and streamline your workflow within the platform.

<Info>
**Looking for more advanced features?** For complete documentation including Security, Linked Accounts, and Account Deletion, see [Platform → User Profile](/platform/user-profile).
</Info>

## Accessing Your Profile

Click on your avatar in the top right corner of any page, then select **"Profile"** from the dropdown menu.

---

## Profile Avatar

Your avatar appears throughout the platform — in the top navigation bar, team member lists, audit logs, and collaboration features.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/upload-avatar.png" alt="Avatar upload interface in Valiyou showing drag-and-drop photo upload with crop tool and file size requirements" />
</Frame>

### Upload Your Avatar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile">
    Click your current avatar (or initials placeholder) → **Profile**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click on Avatar">
    Click the large circular avatar in the **Profile Avatar** section
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose File">
    Select a photo from your computer (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP under 5MB)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crop Photo">
    Use the crop tool to frame your photo:
    - Drag to reposition
    - Scroll to zoom in/out
    - Click **Crop** when ready
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Your new avatar appears immediately across the entire platform. To remove it later, click the avatar again and select **Remove current photo**.
</Tip>

## Personal Information

### Update Your Name

Your display name appears in team lists, audit logs, and system notifications.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/valiyou.com_profile(iPad Pro) (1).png" alt="Personal information form in Valiyou profile settings showing name, email, and language preference fields with save button" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile">
    Click your avatar → **Profile**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit Name">
    Update the **Name** field under Personal Information
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Personal Info** button
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Update Your Email

Your email is used for login, notifications, and password resets.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile">
    Click your avatar → **Profile**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit Email">
    Update the **Email Address** field
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify">
    Check your inbox and click the verification link
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Changing your email affects all organizations you belong to. You'll be logged out and must log in with your new email.
</Warning>

### Change Language

Choose between **English** and **Dansk** (Danish) for all UI text, date formatting, and system emails.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile">
    Click your avatar → **Profile**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Language">
    Choose **English** or **Dansk** from the dropdown
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Personal Info** — the interface updates immediately
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick Troubleshooting

### Avatar Not Updating

If your avatar doesn't update after upload:
1. **Check file size** - Must be under 5MB
2. **Check file format** - Use JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP
3. **Refresh the page** - Click your avatar in the top bar to see the update
4. **Clear browser cache** - If you still see the old avatar

### Email Verification Not Received

If you don't receive the email verification:
1. **Check spam folder** - Verification emails may be filtered
2. **Wait a few minutes** - Email delivery can be delayed
3. **Check email address** - Make sure it's typed correctly
4. **Contact support** - Email support@valiyou.com for help

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What image formats are supported for profile avatars?">
    Valiyou supports JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP formats for profile avatars. Your image file must be under 5MB in size. For best results, use a square image with at least 400x400 pixels resolution. The platform automatically optimizes your image for display across different screen sizes and devices while maintaining quality.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use the same account across multiple organizations?">
    Yes, a single Valiyou account can belong to multiple organizations simultaneously. Each organization maintains separate data, permissions, and billing. You can switch between organizations using the organization selector in the top navigation bar. Your profile information (name, avatar, email) remains consistent across all organizations, but your permissions and roles are organization-specific.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my password?">
    To change your password, navigate to your profile page and scroll to the **Security** section. Click "Change Password," enter your current password for verification, then provide and confirm your new password. Your new password must meet security requirements including minimum length and complexity. For complete password management instructions, see the [User Profile Security](/platform/user-profile#security) documentation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I change my email address?">
    Changing your email address affects all organizations you belong to, as it's your universal login credential. After updating your email, you'll receive a verification link at the new address. You must verify the new email before it becomes active. Once verified, you'll be logged out automatically and must log in using your new email address. All notifications and password reset emails will be sent to the new address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I link multiple login methods to my account?">
    Yes, Valiyou allows you to link multiple authentication methods including Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn, and Salesforce to a single account. This provides flexibility in how you sign in and adds a backup login method if one provider is unavailable. Manage linked accounts from your profile's **Linked Accounts** section. See [Linked Accounts documentation](/platform/user-profile#linked-accounts) for detailed instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does changing my language preference affect my team members?">
    No, language preferences are user-specific and don't affect other team members. When you select English or Dansk (Danish), it only changes the interface language for your account. Each team member can independently choose their preferred language. This ensures international teams can collaborate effectively with each member using their preferred language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I remove my profile avatar?">
    To remove your profile avatar, click on your avatar in the top right corner, select **Profile**, then click the large avatar image in the Profile Avatar section. You'll see an option to "Remove current photo" which will revert your avatar to your initials displayed in a colored circle. You can upload a new avatar at any time using the same process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I recover my account if I forget my password?">
    Yes, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your email address and you'll receive password reset instructions. Click the link in the email to create a new password. The reset link expires after 24 hours for security. If you've linked alternative login methods (Google, Microsoft, etc.), you can also sign in using those providers without needing your password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my avatar showing as initials instead of my photo?">
    If you haven't uploaded a custom avatar, Valiyou automatically displays your initials in a colored circle as a placeholder. This ensures all users have a visible identifier in team lists and collaboration features. To replace the initials with a photo, simply upload an avatar following the steps in the Profile Avatar section above.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I permanently delete my Valiyou account?">
    Account deletion is available in the **Danger Zone** section of your profile settings. Before deleting, consider that this action is permanent after a 30-day grace period and will remove you from all organizations. If you're the sole owner of any organizations, you must transfer ownership or delete those organizations first. For complete deletion instructions and important considerations, see [Account Deletion documentation](/platform/user-profile#danger-zone-delete-account).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
