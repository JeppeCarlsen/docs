Manage your organization settings, including master data, logo, currency, and brand value indicators.

## Accessing Settings

Navigate to **Settings** from your dashboard sidebar to access organization configuration.

The Settings page has three tabs:
- **General** - Organization details, logo, currency, and brand values
- **SSO** - Enterprise Single Sign-On configuration
- **Advanced** - Audit log and danger zone (delete organization)

## General Settings

### Upload Organization Logo

Your logo appears throughout the platform and in reports.

1. Click the **avatar circle** at the top of the Settings page
2. Select an image file (max 2MB)
3. **Crop and adjust** the logo using the crop tool
4. Click "Save" to apply

<Tip>
**Drag & Drop**: You can also drag an image file directly onto the avatar circle to upload.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP. The image will be automatically cropped to a square format.
</Info>

To **remove your logo**:
1. Click the avatar circle with your current logo
2. Click "Delete" in the crop modal
3. Confirm removal

### Organization Details

Update your organization's master data:

- **Organization Name** - Your company or club name
- **Street** - Street address
- **House Number** - Building number
- **Postal Code** - ZIP/postal code
- **City** - City name
- **Country** - Select from EU countries list
- **CVR/VAT Number** - Tax identification number (with country selector)

Click **"Save Club Settings"** to apply changes.

<Warning>
**Permission Required**: You need the "Settings" permission to edit organization details.
</Warning>

### Base Currency

Select your preferred currency for all valuations and financial calculations.

1. Choose from available currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, DKK, etc.)
2. Click **"Save Currency"** to apply

The currency will be used as the default for:
- New valuations
- Sponsorship values
- Financial reports
- Player transfer calculations

<Info>
**Currency Symbol**: The selected currency's symbol (€, $, £, etc.) will appear throughout the platform.
</Info>

### Default Brand Value Indicators

<Note>
**Available for**: Professional and Enterprise plans only
</Note>

Set default values for brand indicators used in new valuations:

- **Popularity** (1-10) - Brand awareness and recognition
- **CSR** (1-10) - Corporate Social Responsibility perception
- **Culture** (1-10) - Brand culture and identity strength
- **Loyalty** (1-10) - Fan and stakeholder loyalty

These defaults are applied when creating new valuations, saving time and ensuring consistency.

## SSO Settings (Enterprise)

<Note>
**Available for**: Enterprise plan only
</Note>

Configure Enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO) for your organization:

- **SAML 2.0** - Industry-standard authentication
- **OAuth 2.0** - Modern authorization protocol
- **OpenID Connect** - Identity layer on OAuth 2.0

SSO allows your team members to log in using your organization's identity provider (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, etc.).

<Info>
**Permission Required**: Admin or SSO management permission required to configure SSO.
</Info>

## Advanced Settings

### Audit Log (Enterprise)

<Note>
**Available for**: Enterprise plan + Admin permission only
</Note>

View a comprehensive record of all data changes in your organization:

- **Who** made changes (user name and email)
- **What** was changed (before/after values)
- **When** changes occurred (precise timestamp)
- **Where** in the system (table and record ID)

The audit log tracks changes to:
- Valuations
- Organization settings
- User permissions
- Player transfers
- All other critical data

Click **"View Audit Log"** to access the full history.

**Use cases:**
- Compliance audits (GDPR, financial regulations)
- Security investigations
- Team activity monitoring
- Data forensics

### Danger Zone: Delete Organization

<Warning>
**Owner Only**: Only the organization owner can delete the organization.
</Warning>

Deleting your organization will:

**Immediately:**
- Cancel your Stripe subscription (at end of billing period)
- Hide the organization from all members
- Send you an offboarding email with reactivation link

**After 30 days (permanent deletion):**
- All valuations and player transfers
- All organization members and invitations
- All settings, SSO providers, and entitlements

<Info>
**30-Day Grace Period**: You can reactivate your organization within 30 days using the link in the offboarding email.
</Info>

**To delete your organization:**

1. Click **"Delete Organisation"** in the Danger Zone
2. Read the warnings carefully
3. Type your **exact organization name** to confirm
4. Click "Delete Organisation"
5. Check your email for the reactivation link (save it!)

<Tip>
**Reactivation**: If you change your mind, simply click the reactivation link in your offboarding email within 30 days.
</Tip>

## Permissions

Different settings require different permission levels:

| Setting | Required Permission |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Organization Details** | Settings permission |
| **Upload Logo** | Settings permission |
| **Base Currency** | Settings permission |
| **Brand Value Indicators** | Settings permission + Professional/Enterprise plan |
| **SSO Configuration** | Admin or SSO permission + Enterprise plan |
| **Audit Log** | Admin permission + Enterprise plan |
| **Delete Organization** | Owner only |

<Info>
If you don't see certain features, check your plan tier and permissions with your organization owner or admin.
</Info>
