# Organization Settings & Configuration

  <img

**Configure your Valiyou organization settings** to brand your sponsorship reports and proposals with your logo, and set the base currency for accurate valuation calculations across all your sports marketing operations.

Get started quickly by uploading your organization logo and selecting your base currency — essential first steps for professionalizing your sponsorship platform.

## Accessing Settings

Navigate to **Platform** → **Organisation** from the sidebar to manage your logo and organization details.

For additional settings like currency and brand values, go to **Platform** → **Settings**.

  Platform](/platform/settings-page/platform) - Brand values: [Platform → Settings →
  Valuations](/platform/settings-page/valuations) - SSO and advanced: [Platform →
  Settings](/platform/settings/sso)
</Info>

## Upload Organization Logo

Your logo appears throughout the Valiyou platform — in sponsorship reports, valuation dashboards, and professional proposals sent to sponsors. Upload your company or club logo to ensure consistent branding across all stakeholder communications.

### How to Upload Your Logo

1. Click the **avatar circle** at the top of the Organisation page
2. Select an image file (max 2MB)
3. **Crop and adjust** the logo using the built-in crop tool
4. Click "Save" to apply your branded logo

<Tip>
  **Drag & Drop**: You can also drag an image file directly onto the avatar circle to upload.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP. The image will be automatically cropped to a square
  format for consistent display.
</Info>

### Where Your Logo Appears

- **Sponsorship valuations** and reports
- **Professional proposals** sent to sponsors
- **Dashboard header** for brand consistency
- **Exported PDF reports** for board presentations
- **Email communications** to stakeholders

### Remove Your Logo

To **remove your logo**:

1. Click the avatar circle with your current logo
2. Click "Delete" in the crop modal
3. Confirm removal

## Base Currency Configuration

Select your preferred currency for all sponsorship valuations and financial calculations. This setting ensures accurate monetary values across all reports, proposals, and analytics in your local currency.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/valiyou.com_platform_settings(iPad Pro) (1).png"
    alt="Base currency selection interface in Valiyou showing EUR, USD, GBP, DKK options for sponsorship valuation calculations and financial reporting"
  />
</Frame>

### How to Set Base Currency

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Settings** → **Platform** tab
2. Choose from available currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, DKK, SEK, NOK, and more)
3. Click **"Save Currency"** to apply platform-wide

### Where Currency is Used

The selected base currency will be applied to:

- **New sponsorship valuations** and CPM calculations
- **Sponsorship package values** and pricing
- **Financial reports** and ROI analytics
- **Player transfer calculations** and market values
- **Invoice generation** and billing
- **Budget forecasting** and revenue projections

<Info>
  **Currency Symbol**: The selected currency's symbol (€, $, £, kr, etc.) will appear throughout the
  platform in all financial displays.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Changing currency**: If you change your base currency after creating valuations, existing
  valuations will retain their original currency. Only new valuations will use the updated currency
  setting.
</Warning>

## Additional Organization Settings

### Organisation Page

Navigate to **Platform** → **Organisation** for:

- **Organization details** (name, legal entity, registration number)
- **Address information** for invoices and contracts
- **VAT/CVR number** for tax compliance
- **Logo upload and management**

### Settings Page

Navigate to **Platform** → **Settings** for advanced configuration:

**Platform Tab** - All plans

- Base currency selection for financial calculations

**Valuations Tab** - Professional/Enterprise only

- Default brand value indicators and metrics
- Custom valuation methodologies

**Sponsorships Tab** - Enterprise only

- Negotiator contribution margin labels
- Sales team commission structures

**Documents Tab** - All plans

- Document templates for proposals and reports
- Custom folder organization

**SSO Tab** - Enterprise only

- SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect configuration
- Enterprise authentication and security

**Advanced Tab** - All plans

- Data export options
- Danger zone: Delete organization

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What image formats are supported for logo upload?">
    Valiyou supports JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP formats for organization logos. The maximum file size is 2MB, and images are automatically cropped to a square format for consistent display across the platform, reports, and proposals.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my base currency after creating valuations?">
  Yes, you can change your base currency at any time in Platform → Settings → Platform. However,
  existing valuations will retain their original currency. Only new valuations created after the
  change will use the updated currency setting. This prevents historical data from being altered.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where does my logo appear in the platform?">
  Your organization logo appears in multiple places: sponsorship valuation reports, professional
  proposals sent to sponsors, the dashboard header, exported PDF reports for board presentations,
  and email communications to stakeholders. This ensures consistent branding across all touchpoints.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What currencies does Valiyou support?">
  Valiyou supports major global currencies including EUR (Euro), USD (US Dollar), GBP (British
  Pound), DKK (Danish Krone), SEK (Swedish Krona), NOK (Norwegian Krone), and many others. The
  currency symbol appears throughout the platform in financial displays and calculations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I have different currencies for different valuations?">
  While you set a base currency for the platform, you can manually adjust the currency for
  individual valuations if needed. However, most organizations find it simpler to maintain one base
  currency for consistency in reporting and analytics.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to configure settings before creating valuations?">
    It's recommended to upload your logo and set your base currency before creating your first valuation. This ensures your reports and proposals are properly branded and use the correct currency from the start. However, you can update these settings at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
