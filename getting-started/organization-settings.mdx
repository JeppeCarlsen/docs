Get started quickly by uploading your organization logo and selecting your base currency for valuations.

## Accessing Settings

Navigate to **Platform** → **Settings** from the sidebar to access full organization settings.

<Info>
This is a quick setup guide. For complete organization settings documentation including address details, brand values, SSO, audit log, and advanced features, see [Platform → Organization Settings](/platform/organization-settings).
</Info>

## Upload Organization Logo

Your logo appears throughout the platform, in reports, and on proposals.

1. Click the **avatar circle** at the top of the Settings page
2. Select an image file (max 2MB)
3. **Crop and adjust** the logo using the crop tool
4. Click "Save" to apply

<Tip>
**Drag & Drop**: You can also drag an image file directly onto the avatar circle to upload.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP. The image will be automatically cropped to a square format.
</Info>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/getting-started/upload-logo.png" alt="Upload Organization Logo" />
</Frame>

To **remove your logo**:
1. Click the avatar circle with your current logo
2. Click "Delete" in the crop modal
3. Confirm removal

## Base Currency

Select your preferred currency for all valuations and financial calculations.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/valiyou.com_platform_settings(iPad Pro) (1).png" alt="Base Currency" />
</Frame>

1. Choose from available currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, DKK, etc.)
2. Click **"Save Currency"** to apply

The currency will be used as the default for:
- New valuations
- Sponsorship values
- Financial reports
- Player transfer calculations

<Info>
**Currency Symbol**: The selected currency's symbol (€, $, £, etc.) will appear throughout the platform.
</Info>

## Additional Settings

The Settings page has three tabs with additional configuration options:

### General Tab
- Organization details (name, address, VAT/CVR number)
- Logo upload
- Base currency selection
- Default brand value indicators (Professional/Enterprise)

### SSO Tab (Enterprise only)
- SAML 2.0 configuration
- OAuth 2.0 setup
- OpenID Connect integration

### Advanced Tab
- Audit log (Enterprise only)
- Danger zone: Delete organization

<Note>
**Full Documentation**: For detailed information about all settings, see [Platform → Organization Settings](/platform/organization-settings).
</Note>
