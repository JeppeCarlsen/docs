### Sports Clubs & Organizations
Manage your entire commercial operation — from calculating jersey sponsorship value to tracking TV exposure across all competitions. Perfect for football clubs, basketball teams, handball organizations, and multi-sport federations looking to professionalize their sponsorship sales and reporting.

Serve multiple clients with white-label reports, standardized valuation methodology, and automated proposal generation. Demonstrate clear ROI to clients and win more sponsorship deals with data-backed insights.

### Brands & Sponsors
Evaluate sponsorship opportunities objectively before investing. Track the actual value delivered by your sports partnerships with transparent CPM calculations and brand exposure tracking across all media channels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do you calculate sponsorship ROI?">
    Valiyou calculates sponsorship ROI using industry-standard CPM (Cost Per Mille) methodology combined with media value calculations. We track brand exposure across TV broadcasts, digital channels, and social media, then convert that exposure into monetary value based on market rates. This gives you transparent, auditable ROI calculations for every sponsor.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What types of organizations use Valiyou?">
    Valiyou is used by sports clubs (football, basketball, handball, ice hockey), sports marketing agencies managing multiple clients, and brands evaluating sponsorship opportunities. From small local clubs to professional organizations, any entity dealing with sponsorship valuation and management can benefit from our platform.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I track TV exposure and social media value?">
    Yes! Valiyou tracks brand exposure across all channels including TV broadcasts (with timestamp data), social media posts, digital advertising, and physical signage. All exposure is converted to monetary value using CPM calculations and industry benchmarks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take to set up?">
    Most organizations are up and running within 5-10 minutes. The platform is designed for immediate use with intuitive workflows. You can create your first sponsorship valuation on day one and start generating professional proposals immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I try Valiyou before purchasing?">
    You can explore a full demo instantly at valiyou.io with no credit card required, or book a personalized demo call with our team. Both options give you access to all core features including valuations, sponsor management, and proposal generation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Valiyou and traditional CRM software?">
    Unlike general CRMs, Valiyou is purpose-built for sponsorship management with built-in valuation calculators, CPM methodology, media tracking, and sports-specific workflows. It combines sponsor relationship management with financial analytics — everything you need in one platform instead of juggling multiple tools.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
