Comprehensive billing management including subscription details, usage monitoring, seat management, extra valuations, payment methods, and invoice history.

## Accessing Billing

Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** from the sidebar to access billing management.

<Note>
**Permission Required**: Billing permission or Admin access required to view and manage billing.
</Note>

## Page Layout

The Billing page has five tabs for comprehensive subscription management:

### Tab Navigation

- **Subscription** - Plan details, add-ons, renewal date
- **Usage** - Seats and valuations usage limits
- **Payment Methods** - Saved credit cards
- **Invoices** - Invoice history and downloads
- **Billing Details** - Company billing information

<Tip>
**Direct Tab Access**: Use URL hash navigation (e.g., `/billing#usage`) to jump directly to a specific tab.
</Tip>

## Subscription Tab

Manage your plan details, add-ons, and subscription renewal.

### Subscription Details Card

**Displays:**
- **Plan Name**: Starter, Professional, or Enterprise
- **Billing Period**: Monthly or Annually
- **Upgrade Button**: Appears for Starter/Professional plans

<Info>
**Upgrade Available**: Starter and Professional plans show an "Upgrade Plan" button to access higher tiers.
</Info>

**Upgrade Process:**
1. Click "Upgrade Plan" button
2. Upgrade Plan modal opens
3. Select new plan tier
4. Confirm upgrade
5. Subscription updated immediately

### Manage Add-ons Card

<Note>
**Feature Availability**: Add-ons available for Professional and Enterprise plans. Starter plans must upgrade to access add-ons.
</Note>

**Available Add-ons:**
- **AI Insights for Reports** - AI-powered insights using LLMs (Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic)
  - Requires Professional or Enterprise plan
  - Price: Varies by currency (e.g., 99 DKK/month)
  - See [Platform → Plugins](/platform/plugins#ai-insights-for-reports) for configuration
- **Cash Flow Visualization (Sankey)** - Interactive flow charts for valuations
  - Available on all plans (Professional/Enterprise)
  - Price: Varies by currency (e.g., 49 DKK/month)
  - See [Platform → Plugins](/platform/plugins#cash-flow-visualization-sankey) for configuration

**Add-on Management:**
1. Click add-on card to select/deselect
2. Selected add-ons show blue ring border
3. Cost info appears showing price breakdown
4. Click "Update Add-ons" to apply changes
5. Pro-rated charges/credits applied immediately

**Visual Indicators:**
- **Ring Border**: Blue ring = selected, gray ring = not selected
- **"Included" Badge**: Enterprise plans show green "Included" badge (add-ons included in plan)
- **Cost Info Box**: Blue info box shows payment details when changes are made

<Tip>
**Enterprise Benefits**: Enterprise plans include all add-ons at no extra cost. The add-ons are automatically enabled.
</Tip>

**Cost Calculation:**
- **Adding add-ons**: Shows new monthly total + immediate pro-rated charge
- **Removing add-ons**: Shows credit for unused time + new monthly total
- **Mixed changes**: Credits and charges both apply

**Example Cost Info:**
```
Your next payment will be 148 DKK per month for add-ons.
You will be charged immediately for the pro-rated amount.
```

<Warning>
**Immediate Charges**: Add-on changes are charged/credited immediately on a pro-rated basis. Next billing cycle reflects the full monthly amount.
</Warning>

### Subscription Renewal Card

Shows next renewal/payment date.

**Displays:**
- **Status**: Active (auto-renews) or Trial
- **Renewal Date**: Next payment date in format "dd. month yyyy"
- **Empty State**: "No active subscription found" if no payment method

**Status Meanings:**
- **Active**: Subscription auto-renews on renewal date
- **Trialing**: Free trial period (auto-converts to paid)
- **No Payment Method**: Entitlements active but no Stripe subscription

## Usage Tab

Monitor team seats and valuation usage limits.

### Plan Usage Limits Card

Visual progress bars showing current usage.

**Team Seats:**
- **Display**: "X / Y" (e.g., "3 / 5")
- **Progress Bar**: Green (<80% used), Blue (≥80% used)
- **Percentage**: "X% used"
- **Calculation**: Active users + pending invitations

**Valuations (this year):**
- **Display**: "X / Y" (e.g., "12 / 24")
- **Progress Bar**: Green (<80% used), Blue (≥80% used)
- **Percentage**: "X% used • Resets [Date]"
- **Reset Date**: One year from entitlements creation (usually subscription start)

<Info>
**Annual Reset**: Valuation limits reset annually based on subscription start date, not calendar year. Purchased extra valuations carry forward.
</Info>

**What Counts Toward Limits:**
- **Seats**: All active users + pending invitations
- **Valuations**: All valuations created this year (Draft + Approved)

**Plan-Specific Limits:**
- **Starter**: 3 seats, 12 valuations/year
- **Professional**: 5 seats, 24 valuations/year
- **Enterprise**: 10 seats, Unlimited valuations

See [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management) for user management.

### Manage Seats Card

Adjust team seat count with real-time cost preview.

**Current Plan Info:**
- **Included Seats**: Base seats in plan (e.g., "5 included in Professional Plan")
- **Additional Seats**: Extra purchased seats (e.g., "• 2 additional")
- **Seat Price**: Cost per additional seat per month

**Team Seats Stepper:**
- **Minus Button**: Decrease seats (disabled if 0 additional seats)
- **Count Display**: Total seats (included + additional)
- **Plus Button**: Increase seats

**Cost Info** (appears when changes made):
- **Upgrade**: Shows new monthly cost + immediate pro-rated charge
- **Downgrade**: Shows credit for unused time + new monthly cost

**Example Cost Info:**
```
Your next payment will be 398 DKK per month for additional seats.
You will be charged immediately for the pro-rated amount.
```

**Action Buttons:**
- **Cancel**: Reset to current seat count
- **Upgrade/Downgrade Button**: Apply changes (e.g., "Upgrade to 7 seats")

<Tip>
**Flexible Scaling**: Add or remove seats anytime. Pro-rated charges/credits apply immediately, and the new monthly cost takes effect on next billing cycle.
</Tip>

<Warning>
**Removing Seats**: You can only decrease additional seats, not included seats. Ensure you have fewer active users than new seat limit before downgrading.
</Warning>

See [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management#seat-limits) for seat management details.

### Purchase Extra Valuations Card

Buy additional one-time valuations beyond annual limit.

<Note>
**One-Time Purchase**: Extra valuations are purchased individually, not on subscription. They never expire and do not reset annually.
</Note>

**Current Plan Info:**
- **Included Valuations**: Base valuations in plan (e.g., "24 included in Professional Plan")
- **Additional Valuations**: Extra purchased valuations (e.g., "• 5 additional")
- **Valuation Price**: Cost per extra valuation (varies by currency)
- **Reset Date**: When next annual allocation becomes available

**Extra Valuations Stepper:**
- **Minus Button**: Decrease extra valuations (disabled if no unused extra valuations)
- **Count Display**: Total valuations (included + additional)
- **Plus Button**: Increase extra valuations

**Decrease Logic:**
- Can only decrease unused extra valuations
- If you've used extra valuations this year, minus button is disabled
- Example: Purchased 5 extra, used 3 → Can only decrease by 2 max

**Cost Info** (appears when changes made):
- **Purchase**: One-time payment amount, charged immediately
- **Credit**: Account credit for unused valuations, usable for future purchases

**Example Cost Info (Purchase):**
```
One-time payment of 1,990 DKK for 2 extra valuations.
Payment will be charged immediately.
```

**Example Cost Info (Credit):**
```
You will receive account credit for the unused valuation.
Credit amount: 995 DKK. This credit can be used for future purchases.
```

**Action Buttons:**
- **Cancel**: Reset to current extra valuation count
- **Purchase/Credit Button**: Apply changes (e.g., "Purchase 2 valuations" or "Get Credit for 1 valuation")

<Tip>
**No Expiration**: Extra valuations never expire. Purchase when needed, use whenever convenient. They don't reset annually like included valuations.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Account Credit**: When you return unused extra valuations, you receive account credit that can be used for future purchases (seats, add-ons, extra valuations).
</Info>

**When to Purchase Extra Valuations:**
- Exceeded annual limit (e.g., 24/24 used)
- Planning major campaign requiring multiple valuations
- Prefer one-time purchase over upgrading plan
- Need flexibility without long-term commitment

See [Valuations → Create Valuation](/valuations/create-valuation) for valuation creation.

## Payment Methods Tab

View and manage saved payment methods.

### Payment Methods Card

**Displays for each payment method:**
- **Card Brand Icon**: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, etc.
- **Card Brand Name**: "Visa", "Mastercard", etc.
- **Last 4 Digits**: "•••• 1234"
- **Expiration Date**: "Expires 12/2025"
- **Default Badge**: Blue "Default" badge if primary payment method

**Actions:**
- **Manage Payment Methods Button**: Opens Stripe Customer Portal
  - Add new payment method
  - Remove old payment method
  - Set default payment method
  - Update card details

**Empty State:**
- Message: "No payment methods found"
- Action: "Add Payment Method" button → Opens Stripe Customer Portal

<Info>
**Stripe Customer Portal**: All payment method management happens via Stripe's secure portal. No card details are stored or handled by Valiyou.
</Info>

**Security Features:**
- Card numbers never fully displayed (only last 4 digits)
- No card details stored in Valiyou database
- PCI DSS compliant via Stripe
- Secure Stripe Customer Portal for all changes

## Invoices Tab

View invoice history and download PDF invoices.

### Invoices Card

**Invoice Table** (desktop):

**Columns:**
- **Invoice**: Invoice number or ID
- **Date**: Invoice date in dd/MM/yyyy format
- **Amount**: Total amount with currency (e.g., "1,295.00 DKK")
- **Status**: Badge showing paid status
  - Green "Paid" badge
  - Yellow badge for "Open" invoices
  - Red badge for failed payments
- **Actions**: "Download PDF" link (if available)

**Invoice Types:**
- **Subscription Invoices**: Monthly/annual plan charges
- **Add-on Invoices**: Add-on subscription changes
- **One-time Invoices**: Extra valuation purchases, seat upgrades
- **Credit Notes**: Refunds and credits

**Actions:**
- **Download PDF**: Click link to download invoice PDF
  - Opens in new tab
  - Suitable for accounting/bookkeeping
  - Contains full invoice details

**Empty State:**
- Message: "No invoices found"
- Displays when no invoices exist yet

<Tip>
**Accounting Integration**: Download PDF invoices for your accounting software or bookkeeping records. All invoices are immediately available after payment.
</Tip>

**Invoice Details** (in PDF):
- Invoice number
- Invoice date
- Billing details (company name, address, VAT)
- Line items with descriptions
- Subtotal, tax, total
- Payment status

## Billing Details Tab

Manage company billing information and tax details.

### Billing Details Card

**Company Information:**
- **Name**: Company or organization name
- **Address**: Full billing address
  - Line 1 (street address)
  - Line 2 (apartment/suite, optional)
  - Postal Code + City
  - Country
- **Email**: Billing contact email
- **VAT Country**: Country for VAT purposes
- **Tax ID**: VAT number or tax identification

**Actions:**
- **Update Billing Details Button**: Opens Stripe Customer Portal
  - Update company name
  - Change billing address
  - Update email address
  - Add/update VAT number
  - Change tax country

**Empty State:**
- Message: "No billing details found"
- Action: "Add Billing Details" button → Opens Stripe Customer Portal

<Info>
**VAT & Tax**: Billing details are used for invoices and tax calculations. Ensure VAT number is correct for EU businesses to avoid incorrect charges.
</Info>

**Why Update Billing Details:**
- Company name changed
- Moved to new office/address
- Need VAT number on invoices
- Change billing contact email
- Update tax country for compliance

<Warning>
**Invoice Accuracy**: Billing details appear on all invoices. Ensure information is accurate before making purchases to avoid invoice corrections.
</Warning>

## Common Use Cases

### Adding Team Members (Purchase Seats)

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Usage** tab
2. Find **Manage Seats** card
3. Click **Plus Button** to increase seat count
4. Review cost info (pro-rated charge + new monthly cost)
5. Click **"Upgrade to X seats"** button
6. Success modal confirms seat addition
7. Go to **Platform** → **Users** to invite new members

See [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management#inviting-users) for invitation process.

### Purchasing Extra Valuations

1. Check current usage: **Billing** → **Usage** tab → **Plan Usage Limits**
2. If at/near limit, go to **Purchase Extra Valuations** card
3. Click **Plus Button** to add extra valuations
4. Review cost info (one-time payment)
5. Click **"Purchase X valuations"** button
6. Payment processed immediately
7. Success modal confirms purchase
8. Create new valuations: **Valuations** → **New Valuation**

See [Valuations → Create Valuation](/valuations/create-valuation) for valuation creation.

### Enabling AI Insights Plugin

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Subscription** tab
2. Find **Manage Add-ons** card
3. Click **AI Insights for Reports** card to select
4. Review cost info (monthly charge + pro-rated amount)
5. Click **"Update Add-ons"** button
6. Success modal confirms add-on enabled
7. Go to **Platform** → **Plugins** to configure AI provider
8. Use AI Insights in **Valuations** → **Reports**

See [Platform → Plugins](/platform/plugins#ai-insights-for-reports) for AI Insights configuration.

### Upgrading Plan Tier

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Subscription** tab
2. Click **"Upgrade Plan"** button (Subscription Details card)
3. Upgrade Plan modal opens
4. Select new plan tier (Professional or Enterprise)
5. Review features and pricing
6. Click **"Upgrade to [Plan]"** button
7. Subscription upgraded immediately
8. New entitlements active (seats, valuations, features)

<Info>
**Pro-rated Charges**: Plan upgrades are pro-rated. You're only charged for the remaining days in your billing cycle at the new plan rate.
</Info>

### Updating Payment Method

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Payment Methods** tab
2. Click **"Manage Payment Methods"** button
3. Stripe Customer Portal opens in new window
4. Add new card or update existing card
5. Set new card as default (if desired)
6. Return to Valiyou (portal auto-redirects)
7. New payment method appears in list

### Downloading Invoices for Accounting

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Invoices** tab
2. Find invoice in table (sorted by date, newest first)
3. Click **"Download PDF"** link in Actions column
4. PDF opens in new tab or downloads
5. Save PDF to accounting folder
6. Import to accounting software (if applicable)

<Tip>
**Bulk Download**: For multiple invoices, open each PDF in new tabs, then save all at once using browser's save multiple tabs feature.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Access Billing Page

**Reasons:**
- No Billing permission
- Not an Admin
- Redirected to Access Denied

**Fix:**
- Request Billing permission from Admin
- Request Admin role from owner
- Contact organization owner for access

### Add-on Not Available

**Reasons:**
- On Starter plan (AI Insights requires Professional/Enterprise)
- Add-on already included in plan (Enterprise)
- Demo mode active

**Fix:**
- Upgrade to Professional/Enterprise plan
- Check if add-on is already enabled (Enterprise plans include all add-ons)
- Upgrade from demo mode to paid plan

### Can't Decrease Seats

**Reasons:**
- No additional seats to remove (only included seats)
- More active users than new seat limit
- Pending invitations count toward limit

**Fix:**
- Remove users first: [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management#removing-users)
- Cancel pending invitations
- Then decrease seats

### Can't Decrease Extra Valuations

**Reasons:**
- No extra valuations purchased
- All extra valuations used this year
- Minus button disabled

**Fix:**
- Can only return unused extra valuations
- Wait until next annual reset
- Purchase more instead of decreasing (if needed)

### Payment Failed

**Reasons:**
- Card expired
- Insufficient funds
- Card blocked by bank
- Incorrect billing details

**Fix:**
- Update payment method: **Payment Methods** tab → **Manage Payment Methods**
- Contact your bank to authorize payment
- Verify billing details: **Billing Details** tab → **Update Billing Details**
- Try again after fixing issue

### Invoices Not Showing

**Reasons:**
- No invoices generated yet
- First subscription just started
- Payment method not added

**Fix:**
- Wait for first billing cycle to complete
- Add payment method: **Payment Methods** tab
- Refresh page after adding payment method

### Cost Info Shows Wrong Currency

**Reasons:**
- Billing currency set in Stripe
- Currency changed after subscription started
- Multiple currencies in account

**Fix:**
- Currency is set by Stripe based on payment method
- Contact support to change billing currency
- New subscription required to change currency

## Subscription Lifecycle

### Subscription Start

**Initial Setup:**
1. Sign up on pricing page
2. Select plan and billing period
3. Enter payment method
4. Billing details collected
5. Subscription activated
6. Entitlements granted immediately

### Active Subscription

**Ongoing Management:**
- Monthly/annual automatic payments
- Add-on changes pro-rated
- Seat adjustments pro-rated
- Extra valuations purchased as needed
- Invoices generated automatically
- Limits reset annually (valuations)

### Subscription Changes

**Upgrade Plan:**
- Pro-rated charge for remaining period
- New entitlements active immediately
- Next billing cycle reflects new plan price

**Add/Remove Add-ons:**
- Pro-rated charge/credit applied
- Add-ons active/inactive immediately
- Next billing cycle reflects changes

**Manage Seats:**
- Pro-rated charge/credit applied
- New seat limit active immediately
- Invite users to fill seats

### Subscription Renewal

**Automatic Renewal:**
- Payment processed on renewal date
- Invoice generated automatically
- Subscription continues uninterrupted
- New billing period starts

**Failed Payment:**
- Payment retry attempted
- Email notification sent
- Subscription may be suspended
- Update payment method to restore

### Subscription Cancellation

**Cancellation Process:**
- Open Stripe Customer Portal (via Manage Payment Methods)
- Cancel subscription
- Access continues until period end
- Entitlements removed after period ends
- Data preserved (can reactivate later)

<Warning>
**Data Preservation**: Canceling subscription does not delete your data. Your valuations, reports, and settings are preserved for 30 days. Reactivate anytime to restore full access.
</Warning>
