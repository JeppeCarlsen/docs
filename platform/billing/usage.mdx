# Usage Tracking & Plan Limits

  <img
    src="/images/valiyou.com_platform_billing(iPad Pro) (2).png"

**Monitor your team seat count and valuation usage limits to optimize your plan capacity.** Track active users, pending invitations, and annual valuation consumption with real-time progress bars. Manage seat limits, purchase additional team members, and buy extra valuations as needed with transparent billing period tracking and pro-rated pricing.

## Plan Usage Limits Card

Visual progress bars showing current usage.

**Team Seats:**

- **Display**: "X / Y" (e.g., "3 / 5")
- **Progress Bar**: Green (less than 80% used), Blue (80% or more used)
- **Percentage**: "X% used"
- **Calculation**: Active users + pending invitations

**Valuations (this year):**

- **Display**: "X / Y" (e.g., "12 / 24")
- **Progress Bar**: Green (less than 80% used), Blue (80% or more used)
- **Percentage**: "X% used • Resets [Date]"
- **Reset Date**: One year from entitlements creation (usually subscription start)

<Info>
  **Annual Reset**: Valuation limits reset annually based on subscription start date, not calendar
  year. Purchased extra valuations carry forward.
</Info>

**What Counts Toward Limits:**

- **Seats**: All active users + pending invitations
- **Valuations**: All valuations created this year (Draft + Approved)

**Plan-Specific Limits:**

- **Starter**: 3 seats, 12 valuations/year
- **Professional**: 5 seats, 24 valuations/year
- **Enterprise**: 10 seats, Unlimited valuations

See [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management) for user management.

## Manage Seats Card

Adjust team seat count with real-time cost preview.

**Current Plan Info:**

- **Included Seats**: Base seats in plan (e.g., "5 included in Professional Plan")
- **Additional Seats**: Extra purchased seats (e.g., "• 2 additional")
- **Seat Price**: Cost per additional seat per month

**Team Seats Stepper:**

- **Minus Button**: Decrease seats (disabled if 0 additional seats)
- **Count Display**: Total seats (included + additional)
- **Plus Button**: Increase seats

**Cost Info** (appears when changes made):

- **Upgrade**: Shows new monthly cost + immediate pro-rated charge
- **Downgrade**: Shows credit for unused time + new monthly cost

**Example Cost Info:**

```
Your next payment will be 398 DKK per month for additional seats.
You will be charged immediately for the pro-rated amount.
```

**Action Buttons:**

- **Cancel**: Reset to current seat count
- **Upgrade/Downgrade Button**: Apply changes (e.g., "Upgrade to 7 seats")

<Tip>
  **Flexible Scaling**: Add or remove seats anytime. Pro-rated charges/credits apply immediately,
  and the new monthly cost takes effect on next billing cycle.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  **Removing Seats**: You can only decrease additional seats, not included seats. Ensure you have
  fewer active users than new seat limit before downgrading.
</Warning>

See [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management#seat-limits) for seat management details.

## Purchase Extra Valuations Card

Buy additional one-time valuations beyond annual limit.

<Note>
  **One-Time Purchase**: Extra valuations are purchased individually, not on subscription. They
  never expire and do not reset annually.
</Note>

**Current Plan Info:**

- **Included Valuations**: Base valuations in plan (e.g., "24 included in Professional Plan")
- **Additional Valuations**: Extra purchased valuations (e.g., "• 5 additional")
- **Valuation Price**: Cost per extra valuation (varies by currency)
- **Reset Date**: When next annual allocation becomes available

**Extra Valuations Stepper:**

- **Minus Button**: Decrease extra valuations (disabled if no unused extra valuations)
- **Count Display**: Total valuations (included + additional)
- **Plus Button**: Increase extra valuations

**Decrease Logic:**

- Can only decrease unused extra valuations
- If you've used extra valuations this year, minus button is disabled
- Example: Purchased 5 extra, used 3 → Can only decrease by 2 max

**Cost Info** (appears when changes made):

- **Purchase**: One-time payment amount, charged immediately
- **Credit**: Account credit for unused valuations, usable for future purchases

**Example Cost Info (Purchase):**

```
One-time payment of 1,990 DKK for 2 extra valuations.
Payment will be charged immediately.
```

**Example Cost Info (Credit):**

```
You will receive account credit for the unused valuation.
Credit amount: 995 DKK. This credit can be used for future purchases.
```

**Action Buttons:**

- **Cancel**: Reset to current extra valuation count
- **Purchase/Credit Button**: Apply changes (e.g., "Purchase 2 valuations" or "Get Credit for 1 valuation")

<Tip>
  **No Expiration**: Extra valuations never expire. Purchase when needed, use whenever convenient.
  They don't reset annually like included valuations.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Account Credit**: When you return unused extra valuations, you receive account credit that can
  be used for future purchases (seats, add-ons, extra valuations).
</Info>

**When to Purchase Extra Valuations:**

- Exceeded annual limit (e.g., 24/24 used)
- Planning major campaign requiring multiple valuations
- Prefer one-time purchase over upgrading plan
- Need flexibility without long-term commitment

See [Valuations → Create Valuation](/valuations/create-valuation) for valuation creation.

## Common Use Cases

### Adding Team Members (Purchase Seats)

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Usage** tab
2. Find **Manage Seats** card
3. Click **Plus Button** to increase seat count
4. Review cost info (pro-rated charge + new monthly cost)
5. Click **"Upgrade to X seats"** button
6. Success modal confirms seat addition
7. Go to **Platform** → **Users** to invite new members

See [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management#inviting-users) for invitation process.

### Purchasing Extra Valuations

1. Check current usage: **Billing** → **Usage** tab → **Plan Usage Limits**
2. If at/near limit, go to **Purchase Extra Valuations** card
3. Click **Plus Button** to add extra valuations
4. Review cost info (one-time payment)
5. Click **"Purchase X valuations"** button
6. Payment processed immediately
7. Success modal confirms purchase
8. Create new valuations: **Valuations** → **New Valuation**

See [Valuations → Create Valuation](/valuations/create-valuation) for valuation creation.

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Decrease Seats

**Reasons:**

- No additional seats to remove (only included seats)
- More active users than new seat limit
- Pending invitations count toward limit

**Fix:**

- Remove users first: [Platform → Team Management](/platform/team-management#removing-users)
- Cancel pending invitations
- Then decrease seats

### Can't Decrease Extra Valuations

**Reasons:**

- No extra valuations purchased
- All extra valuations used this year
- Minus button disabled

**Fix:**

- Can only return unused extra valuations
- Wait until next annual reset
- Purchase more instead of decreasing (if needed)

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I monitor my team seat usage and plan capacity?">
    Navigate to Platform → Billing → Usage tab to view the Plan Usage Limits card. This displays your current team seat count (active users + pending invitations) versus your plan's seat limit, with color-coded progress bars showing usage percentage.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="When do valuation limits reset in my billing period?">
  Valuation limits reset annually based on your subscription start date, not the calendar year. The
  exact reset date is displayed in the Plan Usage Limits card as "Resets [Date]". Purchased extra
  valuations carry forward and never expire.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I purchase additional seats for my team?">
  In the Usage tab, locate the Manage Seats card and click the Plus Button to increase your seat
  count. Review the pro-rated charge and new monthly cost, then click "Upgrade to X seats" to
  complete the purchase. New seats are available immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my plan's valuation limit?">
  When you reach your annual valuation limit, you can purchase extra valuations individually through
  the Purchase Extra Valuations card. These are one-time purchases that never expire and don't reset
  annually, providing flexibility beyond your plan's included valuations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I reduce my team seat count to save money?">
  Yes, you can decrease additional seats (beyond your plan's included seats) anytime. Ensure you
  have fewer active users than the new seat limit before downgrading. You'll receive a pro-rated
  credit for unused time, and the new monthly cost takes effect next billing cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do pending user invitations count toward my seat limit?">
  Yes, pending invitations count toward your team seat usage limit. If you're at capacity, cancel
  unused invitations before sending new ones, or purchase additional seats to accommodate more team
  members.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does pro-rated billing work when adding seats mid-cycle?">
  When you add seats during your billing period, you're charged immediately for the pro-rated amount
  (the cost for remaining days in the current cycle). The full monthly cost for additional seats is
  then included in your next regular billing cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get a refund for unused extra valuations?">
  Yes, you can return unused extra valuations for account credit. Use the minus button in the
  Purchase Extra Valuations card to decrease unused valuations. The credit amount can be used for
  future purchases like seats, add-ons, or more valuations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the difference between included and additional seats?">
  Included seats are part of your base plan (e.g., 5 seats in Professional Plan) at no extra cost.
  Additional seats are purchased beyond your plan's included seats and cost extra per month, with
  pricing displayed in the Manage Seats card.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I track usage across multiple billing periods?">
    The Usage tab shows current billing period statistics with real-time progress bars. For historical tracking, view your invoice history in the Invoices tab, which shows seat counts and valuation purchases for each billing period.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
