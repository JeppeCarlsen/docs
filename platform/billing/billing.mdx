Manage your plan details, add-ons, and subscription renewal.

## Subscription Details Card

**Displays:**
- **Plan Name**: Starter, Professional, or Enterprise
- **Billing Period**: Monthly or Annually
- **Upgrade Button**: Appears for Starter/Professional plans

<Info>
**Upgrade Available**: Starter and Professional plans show an "Upgrade Plan" button to access higher tiers.
</Info>

**Upgrade Process:**
1. Click "Upgrade Plan" button
2. Upgrade Plan modal opens
3. Select new plan tier
4. Confirm upgrade
5. Subscription updated immediately

## Manage Add-ons Card

<Note>
**Feature Availability**: Add-ons available for Professional and Enterprise plans. Starter plans must upgrade to access add-ons.
</Note>

**Available Add-ons:**
- **AI Insights for Reports** - AI-powered insights using LLMs (Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic)
  - Requires Professional or Enterprise plan
  - Price: Varies by currency (e.g., 99 DKK/month)
  - See [Platform → Plugins](/platform/plugins#ai-insights-for-reports) for configuration
- **Cash Flow Visualization (Sankey)** - Interactive flow charts for valuations
  - Available on all plans (Professional/Enterprise)
  - Price: Varies by currency (e.g., 49 DKK/month)
  - See [Platform → Plugins](/platform/plugins#cash-flow-visualization-sankey) for configuration

**Add-on Management:**
1. Click add-on card to select/deselect
2. Selected add-ons show blue ring border
3. Cost info appears showing price breakdown
4. Click "Update Add-ons" to apply changes
5. Pro-rated charges/credits applied immediately

**Visual Indicators:**
- **Ring Border**: Blue ring = selected, gray ring = not selected
- **"Included" Badge**: Enterprise plans show green "Included" badge (add-ons included in plan)
- **Cost Info Box**: Blue info box shows payment details when changes are made

<Tip>
**Enterprise Benefits**: Enterprise plans include all add-ons at no extra cost. The add-ons are automatically enabled.
</Tip>

**Cost Calculation:**
- **Adding add-ons**: Shows new monthly total + immediate pro-rated charge
- **Removing add-ons**: Shows credit for unused time + new monthly total
- **Mixed changes**: Credits and charges both apply

**Example Cost Info:**
```
Your next payment will be 148 DKK per month for add-ons.
You will be charged immediately for the pro-rated amount.
```

<Warning>
**Immediate Charges**: Add-on changes are charged/credited immediately on a pro-rated basis. Next billing cycle reflects the full monthly amount.
</Warning>

## Subscription Renewal Card

Shows next renewal/payment date.

**Displays:**
- **Status**: Active (auto-renews) or Trial
- **Renewal Date**: Next payment date in format "dd. month yyyy"
- **Empty State**: "No active subscription found" if no payment method

**Status Meanings:**
- **Active**: Subscription auto-renews on renewal date
- **Trialing**: Free trial period (auto-converts to paid)
- **No Payment Method**: Entitlements active but no Stripe subscription

## Common Use Cases

### Enabling AI Insights Plugin

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Subscription** tab
2. Find **Manage Add-ons** card
3. Click **AI Insights for Reports** card to select
4. Review cost info (monthly charge + pro-rated amount)
5. Click **"Update Add-ons"** button
6. Success modal confirms add-on enabled
7. Go to **Platform** → **Plugins** to configure AI provider
8. Use AI Insights in **Valuations** → **Reports**

See [Platform → Plugins](/platform/plugins#ai-insights-for-reports) for AI Insights configuration.

### Upgrading Plan Tier

1. Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** → **Subscription** tab
2. Click **"Upgrade Plan"** button (Subscription Details card)
3. Upgrade Plan modal opens
4. Select new plan tier (Professional or Enterprise)
5. Review features and pricing
6. Click **"Upgrade to [Plan]"** button
7. Subscription upgraded immediately
8. New entitlements active (seats, valuations, features)

<Info>
**Pro-rated Charges**: Plan upgrades are pro-rated. You're only charged for the remaining days in your billing cycle at the new plan rate.
</Info>

## Subscription Lifecycle

### Subscription Start

**Initial Setup:**
1. Sign up on pricing page
2. Select plan and billing period
3. Enter payment method
4. Billing details collected
5. Subscription activated
6. Entitlements granted immediately

### Active Subscription

**Ongoing Management:**
- Monthly/annual automatic payments
- Add-on changes pro-rated
- Seat adjustments pro-rated
- Extra valuations purchased as needed
- Invoices generated automatically
- Limits reset annually (valuations)

### Subscription Changes

**Upgrade Plan:**
- Pro-rated charge for remaining period
- New entitlements active immediately
- Next billing cycle reflects new plan price

**Add/Remove Add-ons:**
- Pro-rated charge/credit applied
- Add-ons active/inactive immediately
- Next billing cycle reflects changes

**Manage Seats:**
- Pro-rated charge/credit applied
- New seat limit active immediately
- Invite users to fill seats

### Subscription Renewal

**Automatic Renewal:**
- Payment processed on renewal date
- Invoice generated automatically
- Subscription continues uninterrupted
- New billing period starts

**Failed Payment:**
- Payment retry attempted
- Email notification sent
- Subscription may be suspended
- Update payment method to restore

### Subscription Cancellation

**Cancellation Process:**
- Open Stripe Customer Portal (via Manage Payment Methods)
- Cancel subscription
- Access continues until period end
- Entitlements removed after period ends
- Data preserved (can reactivate later)

<Warning>
**Data Preservation**: Canceling subscription does not delete your data. Your valuations, reports, and settings are preserved for 30 days. Reactivate anytime to restore full access.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

### Add-on Not Available

**Reasons:**
- On Starter plan (AI Insights requires Professional/Enterprise)
- Add-on already included in plan (Enterprise)
- Demo mode active

**Fix:**
- Upgrade to Professional/Enterprise plan
- Check if add-on is already enabled (Enterprise plans include all add-ons)
- Upgrade from demo mode to paid plan
