Comprehensive billing management including subscription details, usage monitoring, seat management, extra valuations, payment methods, and invoice history.

## Accessing Billing

Navigate to **Platform** → **Billing** from the sidebar to access billing management.

<Note>
**Permission Required**: Billing permission or Admin access required to view and manage billing.
</Note>

## Page Layout

The Billing page has five tabs for comprehensive subscription management:

### Tab Navigation

- **Subscription** - Plan details, add-ons, renewal date
- **Usage** - Seats and valuations usage limits
- **Payment Methods** - Saved credit cards
- **Invoices** - Invoice history and downloads
- **Billing Details** - Company billing information

<Tip>
**Direct Tab Access**: Use URL hash navigation (e.g., `/billing#usage`) to jump directly to a specific tab.
</Tip>

## Permissions

Access to billing features requires specific permissions:

| Permission | Can View Subscription | Can Manage Add-ons | Can Manage Seats | Can Purchase Valuations | Can View Invoices | Can Update Billing Details |
|------------|----------------------|-------------------|-----------------|----------------------|------------------|---------------------------|
| **Admin** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Billing** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Other Roles** | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No |

<Note>
**Billing-Only Access**: You can grant team members Billing permission without other roles. They can manage subscription but not access valuations/reports.
</Note>
