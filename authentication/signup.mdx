# Sign Up & Create Free Account

  <img src="/images/valiyou.com_invite_3f3d0e3e-c69c-4695-8a8b-da2740ad08ad(iPad Pro)Create-your-account.png" alt="Valiyou account creation page showing user registration form with email, password, and social sign-up options including Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft, and Salesforce" />

**Create your free Valiyou account in minutes and unlock powerful sponsorship management tools.** User registration on Valiyou is simple and secure, offering multiple account creation methods including email and password or instant social sign-up with Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft, and Salesforce. Start managing your brand valuations and sponsorships today with our 14-day free trial and comprehensive onboarding process.

## Sign Up Methods

### Email and Password

**Standard Registration**:
1. Navigate to [valiyou.com/auth/signup](https://valiyou.com/auth/signup)
2. Enter your **full name**
3. Enter your **email address**
4. Create a **password** (minimum 8 characters)
5. Accept **Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy**
6. Click **"Sign up"**
7. Check your inbox for verification email

<Warning>
**Email Verification Required**: You must verify your email address before you can log in and access the platform.
</Warning>

### Social Authentication

Create account instantly with existing credentials:

**Available Providers**:
- **Google** - Sign up with Google account
- **LinkedIn** - Sign up with LinkedIn account
- **Microsoft** - Sign up with Microsoft account
- **Salesforce** - Sign up with Salesforce account

**How to Use**:
1. Click provider button (e.g., "Continue with Google")
2. Authorize Valiyou to access basic profile info
3. Account created automatically
4. No email verification needed (already verified by provider)
5. Redirected to onboarding

<Info>
**Auto-Verification**: Social sign-ups are automatically verified since the provider has already confirmed your email.
</Info>

## After Sign Up

### Email Verification

1. Open the verification email from **Valiyou** (`support@valiyou.com`)
2. Click **"Verify Email"** button
3. Redirected to Valiyou with confirmed account
4. Ready to log in

<Tip>
**Didn't Receive Email?** Check spam folder. Email should arrive within 1-2 minutes.
</Tip>

See [Verify Email](/authentication/verify-email) for troubleshooting.

### Onboarding Process

After verification, you'll be guided through:

1. **Welcome Screen** - Introduction to Valiyou
2. **Organization Setup** - Create or join an organization
3. **Plan Selection** - Choose Starter, Professional, or Enterprise
4. **Add-ons** (optional) - Select additional features
5. **Payment** - Enter billing information
6. **Summary** - Review and confirm

<Note>
**Free Trial**: All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required for Starter plan.
</Note>

See [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start) for onboarding details.

## Accepting Invitations

If you received an invitation email:

1. Click invitation link in email
2. Automatically directed to sign-up page
3. Email pre-filled from invitation
4. Create account with **exact email from invitation**
5. After verification, automatically added to organization

<Warning>
**Email Must Match**: Use the exact email address that received the invitation. Different emails won't be accepted.
</Warning>

See [Invite Team Members](/getting-started/invite-team-members) for details.

## Password Requirements

Your password must meet these criteria:

- **Minimum**: 8 characters
- **Maximum**: No limit
- **Allowed**: Letters, numbers, special characters
- **Case-sensitive**: `Password` ≠ `password`

<Tip>
**Best Practice**: Use a password manager to generate strong, unique passwords for each service.
</Tip>

**Strong Password Examples**:
- ✅ `MyV@li!You2025`
- ✅ `Sponsorship$Tracking#2024`
- ❌ `password123` (too weak)
- ❌ `valiyou` (too short)

## Troubleshooting

### Email Already Registered

**Error**: "An account with this email already exists"

**Causes**:
- Already signed up previously
- Signed up with social provider

**Solutions**:
- Try [logging in](/authentication/login) instead
- Use social login if you signed up that way
- Click "Forgot password" if you can't remember credentials

### Email Not Received

**Issue**: Verification email didn't arrive

**Solutions**:
1. **Check spam folder** - Most common issue
2. **Wait 5 minutes** - May be delayed
3. **Check email address** - Verify no typos
4. **Request new email** - Contact support
5. **Use different email** - Try alternative address

### Invalid Email Format

**Error**: "Please enter a valid email address"

**Causes**:
- Missing @ symbol
- Spaces in email
- Invalid domain

**Solutions**:
- Check for typos
- Use format: `name@domain.com`
- No spaces or special characters (except + - . _)

### Password Too Weak

**Error**: "Password must be at least 8 characters"

**Solution**: Use minimum 8 characters with mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.

### Terms Not Accepted

**Error**: "You must accept the terms to continue"

**Solution**: Check the "I agree to Terms of Use and Privacy Policy" checkbox before clicking Sign up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a new Valiyou account?">
    Creating a Valiyou account is simple: navigate to valiyou.com/auth/signup, enter your full name, email address, and create a password (minimum 8 characters), accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, then click "Sign up". Alternatively, use instant social sign-up with Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft, or Salesforce for faster account creation without email verification.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is email verification required after account registration?">
    Yes, if you sign up with email and password, you must verify your email address before accessing the platform. Check your inbox for a verification email from support@valiyou.com and click the "Verify Email" button. Social sign-ups (Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft, Salesforce) are automatically verified and don't require this step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sign up with my Google or LinkedIn account?">
    Absolutely! Valiyou supports instant social sign-up with Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft, and Salesforce accounts. Click your preferred provider button, authorize Valiyou to access your basic profile information, and your account is created automatically with no email verification needed. This is the fastest way to get started.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the password requirements for Valiyou accounts?">
    Your password must be at least 8 characters long with no maximum limit. You can use letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords are case-sensitive. For best security, use a password manager to generate strong, unique passwords like "MyV@li!You2025" or "Sponsorship$Tracking#2024".
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Valiyou offer a free trial or free plan?">
    Yes! Valiyou offers a free Starter plan with no credit card required. All paid plans (Professional and Enterprise) include a 14-day free trial so you can explore advanced features risk-free before committing to a subscription.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I accept an invitation to join an organization?">
    Click the invitation link in your email, which automatically directs you to the sign-up page with your email pre-filled. Create your account using the exact email address that received the invitation. After email verification, you'll be automatically added to the organization with the appropriate permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I sign up with an email that's already registered?">
    If you see "An account with this email already exists", it means you've already created an account or signed up with a social provider. Try logging in instead, use the social login method you used during signup, or click "Forgot password" to reset your credentials and regain access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure when creating a Valiyou account?">
    Yes, Valiyou implements enterprise-grade security: passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text, all data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption, email addresses are never shared with third parties, the platform is GDPR compliant, and reCAPTCHA v3 protects against automated bot attacks during registration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What information does Valiyou collect during sign-up?">
    Required information includes your full name, email address, and password (for email signup). Social sign-ups may optionally include your profile picture and work organization. Valiyou never collects payment information until you upgrade plans, social security numbers, or unnecessary personal data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I do if I don't receive the verification email?">
    First, check your spam or junk folder (most common issue). Wait 5 minutes as emails may be delayed. Verify your email address has no typos. If still not received, contact support to request a new verification email, or try creating an account with a different email address.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
