Email verification confirms your email address and activates your Valiyou account.

## Verification Process

### After Sign Up

1. Complete sign-up form
2. See **"Check your email"** confirmation page
3. Open email from **Valiyou** (`support@valiyou.com`)
4. Subject: **"Verify your email address for Valiyou"**
5. Click **"Verify Email"** button
6. Redirected to Valiyou with confirmed account
7. Ready to log in

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/authentication/valiyou-verify-email.png" alt="Verification Email" />
</Frame>

<Info>
**Verification Link**: Valid for 24 hours. Request new email if expired.
</Info>

### What the Email Contains

The verification email includes:

- **Your email address** - Confirms recipient
- **Verify button** - One-click verification
- **Backup link** - Manual copy-paste option
- **Expiration notice** - 24-hour validity
- **Support contact** - Help if issues arise

<Warning>
**Phishing Protection**: Verify sender is `support@valiyou.com`. Never click suspicious verification links.
</Warning>

## Verification States

### Already Verified

**Page**: "Check your email"

**When Shown**:
- Just completed sign-up
- Waiting for verification
- Haven't clicked verification link yet

**Action**: Check your inbox and click the verification link.

### Successfully Verified

**Page**: Automatically redirected to login

**When Shown**:
- Clicked verification link
- Email confirmed successfully
- Account activated

**Action**: Log in with your credentials.

### Verification Expired

**Error**: "Verification link has expired"

**When Shown**:
- More than 24 hours since sign-up
- Verification link no longer valid

**Solution**: Contact support to request new verification email.

## Troubleshooting

### Email Not Received

**Common Causes**:
1. **Spam folder** - Check junk/spam folders (most common)
2. **Wrong email** - Verify email address during sign-up
3. **Delay** - Can take 1-5 minutes to arrive
4. **Email filters** - Overly aggressive spam filters

**Solutions**:
1. **Check spam** - Look in spam, junk, promotions folders
2. **Wait** - Give it 5-10 minutes
3. **Whitelist sender** - Add `support@valiyou.com` to contacts
4. **Check email** - Verify you used correct address
5. **Contact support** - Request manual verification

<Tip>
**Prevent Spam Filtering**: Add `support@valiyou.com` to your email contacts before signing up.
</Tip>

### Link Already Used

**Error**: "This verification link has already been used"

**Cause**: Already verified your email

**Solution**: Go to [login page](/authentication/login) and sign in normally.

### Invalid Link

**Error**: "Invalid verification link"

**Causes**:
- Link corrupted (email client reformatted it)
- Link incomplete (didn't copy full URL)
- Link tampered with

**Solutions**:
1. Click link directly from email (don't copy-paste)
2. If copying, ensure entire URL is copied
3. Contact support for new verification email

### Link Expired

**Error**: "Verification link has expired"

**Cause**: More than 24 hours passed since sign-up

**Solution**: Contact support via [trust-center](https://valiyou.com/trust-center) to request new verification email.

### Wrong Email Address

**Issue**: Signed up with wrong email, can't access inbox

**Solutions**:
1. **Check all email accounts** - Verify which one you used
2. **Contact support** - Can update email address before verification
3. **Sign up again** - Create new account with correct email

<Warning>
**Cannot Change Email**: After verification, email becomes permanent account identifier. Contact support for email changes.
</Warning>

## Social Sign-Up (No Verification)

If you signed up with social provider (Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft, Salesforce):

- **No verification needed** ✅
- Email already verified by provider
- Immediate account activation
- Skip verification step entirely

<Info>
**Instant Access**: Social sign-ups are ready to use immediately after account creation.
</Info>

## Security & Privacy

### Why Verification Matters

1. **Proves ownership** - Confirms you own the email
2. **Prevents spam** - Stops fake account creation
3. **Account recovery** - Enables password reset
4. **Security** - Protects against unauthorized access
5. **Communication** - Ensures you receive important updates

### Verification Link Security

- **Unique token** - Each link is single-use
- **Time-limited** - Expires after 24 hours
- **Encrypted** - Secure HTTPS transmission
- **No password** - Never includes sensitive info

### What We Never Do

- ❌ Ask for password in email
- ❌ Request payment info
- ❌ Include attachments
- ❌ Send verification from different domains
- ❌ Ask you to reply to email

## Email Provider Tips

### Gmail

- Check **Spam** folder
- Check **Promotions** tab
- Add `support@valiyou.com` to contacts

### Outlook/Microsoft

- Check **Junk Email** folder
- Add sender to **Safe Senders** list
- Check **Focused/Other** inbox tabs

### Yahoo

- Check **Spam** folder
- Add to **Contacts**
- Check **Promotions** category

### Corporate Email

- Contact IT if emails blocked
- Request whitelist for `support@valiyou.com`
- May need approval for external emails

## Manual Verification Request

If you cannot find the verification email:

1. Contact support via [trust-center](https://valiyou.com/trust-center)
2. Provide:
   - Email address used for sign-up
   - Date/time of registration
   - Any error messages received
3. Support will manually verify or resend email

<Warning>
**Security Check**: Support may ask security questions to verify your identity before manual verification.
</Warning>
